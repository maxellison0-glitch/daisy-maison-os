## Lesson of the Day - confidential for Max only, not Daryl

Price competitiveness and query intent in Google Shopping: why a surging search term can still be worthless.

The title tracker shows `teacher gifts` at 76.4 impressions/day and `personalised teacher gifts` at 50.5 impressions/day, but together they have produced 0 conversions from 14 clicks over 12 days. That is the key lesson: impressions alone do not prove demand.

In Google Shopping, the shopper sees your product card with its price beside competitors. A broad query like `teacher gifts` often means the shopper is browsing cheap generic ideas. Daisy's personalised GBP 15-30 products can win the impression but lose the click or sale because the intent is too broad or the price comparison is harsh.

Read search terms as a funnel, not a single number. High impressions plus low CTR plus zero conversions means the problem is downstream of the ad. The answer is not a higher bid. Either add the term as a negative if it is wasting spend, or accept the free reach and leave it alone. Wedding terms are different because impressions and conversions move together there.
