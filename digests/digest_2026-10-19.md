## 📚 Lesson of the Day *(confidential: Max only, not Daryl)*

**Merchant Center promotions: putting the offer on the Shopping ad itself**

*This builds on Merchant Center feed health and yesterday's seasonality adjustments.* A **promotion** in Merchant Center (merchant.google.com → Marketing → Promotions) attaches a discount code or free-delivery offer to your products. It then shows as a small "Special offer" link under the price in Shopping ads. Google checks it against the store, usually within 24 hours, so set it up a day before the offer starts. The mechanism: Shopping ads sit in a grid next to competitors with very similar pictures. The offer label is often the only thing that sets yours apart, so more people click (**CTR**, the share of people who see the ad and click it). Your bid doesn't change. How it fails: the code ends on the site but the promotion stays live, and Google disapproves it. Or it's never added, so the offer never shows up where it would matter. Meta has no equivalent, so put the offer in the creative. TikTok's offer labels only work inside TikTok Shop.

**Worked example (18 Oct):** search sessions 216 vs 306 average (−29%), while email/direct jumped 38%. The free-delivery weekend reached people who already know you, not people searching on Google.

**Ask Daryl this:** "Did FREEDELIVERY25 go into Merchant Center as a promotion this weekend? If not, will Black Friday's?" Good answer: yes, with dates. Evasive answer: "the CSS handles that".

**You should now be able to** make sure every future offer shows on Google, not only in email.
