## 📚 Lesson of the Day

**The search terms report and negative keywords: steering Shopping and PMax**

*Builds on Branded vs non-branded search and Clicks are not visits.* Shopping and PMax campaigns have **no keywords**. Google reads your **product feed** (titles and descriptions) and decides which searches to show you on. The **search terms report** lists the actual words people typed before clicking. A **negative keyword** is a word you ban: "free", "template" or "DIY" stops your ads from showing on any search containing it. Negatives are the main steering wheel you have, because Google picks the searches. **From the outside, the failure looks like this:** clicks and CPC look normal, but conversion rate drifts down, because you're paying for people who were never going to buy a £20 personalised sign.

**Worked example (an illustration, not measured):** ALL OTHER CSS bought 198 clicks at £0.50. If 1 click in 10 came from a junk search, that's 20 × £0.50 = £10/day, or about £70/week, gone.

**TikTok:** there's no real equivalent, because TikTok targets people, not searches.

**Ask Daryl this:** "When did you last go through the search terms for PMAX AUG and ALL OTHER CSS, and what were the last five negatives you added?" A good answer gives a date and actual words. An evasive one is "PMax handles that automatically."

**You should now be able to** check whether Google is spending on the right searches.
