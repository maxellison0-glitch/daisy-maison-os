## 📚 Lesson of the Day *(confidential: Max only, not Daryl)*

**Promo pull-forward: judge the offer on the whole window, not its best day**

*This builds on seasonality adjustments (18 Oct) and day-of-week baselines.* A time-limited offer ("free delivery ends midnight") does two things. It wins some new buyers, and it pulls forward people who would have bought anyway, a few days later. That borrowed demand is called **pull-forward**, and the dip after it is the **hangover**. So the offer days look better than they really were, and the day after looks worse. Both ad platforms make it noisier: Smart Bidding and Meta see **conversion rate** (the share of visits that buy) jump and then drop, and they adjust bids a step behind. The honest read is the whole window, offer days plus hangover, against the same days the week before.

**Worked example:** Sat–Mon 17–19 Oct made £2,813.62 on £910.49 ads = **3.09x**. Sat–Mon 10–12 Oct made £2,938.28 on £1,360.06 = 2.16x. That's sales −4.2% on −33% spend (ads only; the two sends' ~£74 est. would take it to 2.86x). Monday's 1.70x on its own hides that.

**What to watch for:** a sales dip and abandonment spike 1–2 days after every offer. Claude judges offers on the full window. TikTok: same mechanics.

**You should now be able to** judge an offer by its net result, not by its loudest day.
