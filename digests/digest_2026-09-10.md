## 📚 Lesson of the Day: Signal Reversal at Low Purchase Volumes

When an ad set generates only 0–3 purchases per day, the daily CPP can reverse completely from one day to the next through random allocation alone — yesterday ai_images had 3 purchases at £6.42 CPP while product_images had zero; today product_images has 2 at £7.27 while ai_images has zero, on similar spend. This is noise from a sample too small to carry signal. The fix is to judge ad sets on 3-day rolling CPP (minimum ~10 purchases) and only act on sustained divergence, never single-day reads. When TOF-Diffusers expands to 6 ad sets on ~2 daily purchases, every ad set is permanently below the signal threshold — which is the consolidation problem (7 Sep lesson) viewed from the measurement side.
