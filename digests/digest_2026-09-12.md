## 📚 Lesson of the Day

**Boosted Organic Posts vs Purpose-Built Ads.** The new [RDD] Organic posts campaign takes existing content from the Daisy Maison Facebook page and puts ad spend behind it — what Facebook calls "boosting." The advantage is social proof: likes, comments, and shares from organic reach stay visible when the post goes paid, making it look more trusted to strangers than a dark post (an ad-only creative no one has engaged with). The risk is that organic content is designed for followers who already know you, not cold audiences who have never heard of Daisy Maison — a behind-the-scenes reel entertains followers but may not sell to strangers. At £11.15/52 clicks/£0.21 CPC yesterday with 0 purchases, the traffic is the cheapest in the account but conversion data needs 5–7 days before any verdict. TikTok equivalent: Spark Ads (boosted organic TikTok posts that retain original engagement metrics).
