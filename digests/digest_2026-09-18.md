## 📚 Lesson of the Day

**Budget Utilisation Rate — reading how much of a campaign's daily budget is actually spent.** Yesterday TOF-WEDDING spent £154 against its £150/day budget (103% — Facebook allows up to 125%), meaning the algorithm found enough buyers at the current bid; CHRISTMAS STATIC POSTS spent only £9.56 of its £15 (64%), which is the Min ROAS bid strategy correctly throttling when it cannot find impressions that meet the ROAS floor; Social Engagement spent £21.88 of £22 (99.5%), full delivery. When you take over, this is your first daily health check: a campaign spending well under its budget is either throttled by bid strategy (correct and intended) or starved of audience (a problem) — the bid strategy column tells you which.
