## Lesson of the Day - Confidential for Max Only

### CPM versus CPC: price of attention versus price of action

CPM is cost per 1,000 impressions. It tells you how expensive it is to buy attention. CPC is cost per click. It tells you how expensive it is to get a person onto the site.

Yesterday Facebook spent GBP 206.88 for 47,549 impressions and 490 clicks. That is about GBP 4.35 CPM and GBP 0.42 CPC. Google spent GBP 353.09 for 48,046 impressions and 484 clicks, about GBP 7.35 CPM and GBP 0.73 CPC.

So Facebook bought cheaper attention and cheaper clicks, but Shopify still decides the truth: the whole store did GBP 1,350.75 on GBP 559.97 spend, a 2.41x ROAS. Cheap clicks are useful only if they become orders. Yesterday's funnel was healthy, so the lesson is not "Facebook bad" or "Google bad"; it is that CPC and CPM diagnose traffic cost, while ROAS diagnoses whether that traffic met commercial demand.
