## 📚 Lesson of the Day (confidential - not for Daryl)

**Category exhaustion versus category taper.** A seasonal product line doesn't die all at once - it tapers (fewer order-lines each day, like teacher's slide from ~25 lines on 8 Jul to ~1-4 lines by 20-21 Jul) before it hits a true floor: a full day with zero visible order-lines. The taper tells you demand is fading; the zero-line day tells you the window has actually closed. That distinction matters for ad spend because the correct action changes at each stage - reduce budget gradually during the taper (what happened here through most of July), then fully cut once the floor arrives, rather than leaving a residual budget running against an empty demand pool out of habit.

Applied to yesterday: teacher had zero visible order-lines for the first time in this tracked run, and separately the Teacher Gifts campaign has now vanished entirely from both Google and Facebook's campaign lists - the ad-spend cut already caught up with the demand floor, which is the correct sequencing. The blended Title Optimisation Tracker numbers (teacher still "up 176%" on reach) look like a healthy category from a 26-day average, but a single zero-line day underneath that average is the real, current state - a reminder that a rolling window can hide the exact day a category actually ends.
