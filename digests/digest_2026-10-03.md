## 📚 Lesson of the Day

**Clicks are not visits: Clicks (all), link clicks, and landing page views.** This builds on CPC vs CPM (17 Jul) and traffic quality / the cheap-click trap.

- **What "clicks" really counts.** On Meta, the default **Clicks (all)** counts every tap on the ad: likes, comments, profile taps, "see more," expanding the image, and the link. Only a **link click** is a tap that tries to leave Facebook for your site. A **landing page view (LPV)** is a link click where your page actually loaded and the pixel fired. Each step loses people to accidental taps, slow mobile pages, and back-button exits before load.
- **Why it matters at the controls.** A CPC built on Clicks (all) makes a campaign look cheaper than it is. A creative that gets lots of thumb-stopping taps (Reels, carousels, pet videos) inflates Clicks (all) without sending anyone to the store. The cost that matters is **cost per landing page view**, followed by CPP.

**Worked example (2 Oct):** Windsor's Facebook `clicks` field totalled **393** across all ad sets. Shopify recorded only **113** Facebook + Instagram sessions, and some of those were organic. So at most **~29%** of Facebook "clicks" became a site visit (113 ÷ 393). Google is tighter because a Shopping click *is* a link click: 508 clicks against 339 Google sessions, 67%. Pet sign's headline CPC of £0.25 (£28.11 ÷ 114) becomes **at least ~£0.86 per real visit** if it landed at the account's 29% rate (£28.11 ÷ ~33). Its "cheapest click in the account" title disappears.

**At the keyboard:** Ads Manager → Columns → Customise → add **Link clicks**, **CPC (cost per link click)**, **Landing page views** and **Cost per landing page view**. Save it into your custom-column preset. A link-click-to-LPV rate below ~70% points to a slow or broken landing page.

**Ask Daryl this:** "What's Pet sign's link-click-to-landing-page-view rate, and how does it compare with NEW-HOT?" A good answer gives two percentages and says what he'll do if Pet sign's is low. An evasive answer is "the CPC is great."

TikTok draws the same distinction: **Clicks (all)** vs **Clicks (destination)**.

**You should now be able to** tell when a cheap CPC is a fake bargain, and set up Ads Manager to show cost per real visit.
