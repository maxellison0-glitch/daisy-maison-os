## 📚 Lesson of the Day

**Cross-Platform Attribution Overlap.** Facebook claimed 26 purchases yesterday; Google's PMax and Shopping campaigns also attribute purchases from their own click windows. But there were only 37 real Shopify orders. Both platforms independently credit themselves for the same buyer who clicked a Google ad on Monday and a Facebook ad on Tuesday before purchasing Wednesday — each scores it as "their" conversion. This is why adding up platform-reported purchases always exceeds Shopify's order count, and why Shopify total_sales ÷ combined spend is the only honest ROAS. When you run ads in-house, never sum platform ROAS numbers — use Shopify as the single source of truth, exactly as this digest does.
