## Lesson of the Day — Attribution Windows

Attribution windows define the period after someone clicks (or views) your ad during which a subsequent purchase gets credited to that ad. Facebook defaults to 7-day click / 1-day view; Google defaults to 30-day click — so Google claims purchases for 4× longer, which is partly why its ROAS always looks better. When you compare platform-reported ROAS across Google and Facebook, you are comparing different-length rulers. This matters operationally: shifting budget toward the platform with "higher ROAS" may be chasing a measurement artefact rather than real efficiency. *This lesson is confidential for Max.*
