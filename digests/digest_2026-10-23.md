## 📚 Lesson of the Day *(confidential: Max only, not Daryl)*

**New-customer CPA: what an ad pound actually buys**

*This builds on Purchaser exclusions.* Those keep a prospecting campaign from re-buying people who already bought. This is how you check whether that's working. **CPA** (cost per acquisition) is ad spend ÷ orders. **Blended CPA** counts every order. **New-customer CPA** (nCAC) counts only first-time buyers, ÷ new customers. Returning buyers come back through email, bookmarks and word of mouth. Ads take the credit for them anyway, so blended CPA flatters the ads. The failure looks like this from outside: blended CPA holds steady while new customers shrink. The ads are recycling old buyers, not growing the base.

**Worked example (22 Oct):** £322.65 ÷ 33 orders = £9.78 blended. £322.65 ÷ 28 new customers = **£11.52 nCAC**. That's an 18% gap. It's healthy, because 85% of buyers were new.

**What to watch for:** nCAC rising more than 25% above blended CPA, or new customers under 75% of buyers, on a non-email day. Claude flags it. TikTok reports no new/returning split, so use Shopify.

**You should now be able to** say whether the ads are growing the customer base or rebuying the old one.
