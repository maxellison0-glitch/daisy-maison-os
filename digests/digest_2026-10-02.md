## 📚 Lesson of the Day

**Advantage+ Sales campaigns (formerly Advantage+ Shopping): Meta's version of PMax.** This builds on Google PMax (7 Jun) and account consolidation / Learning Limited.

- **What it is.** One campaign with no manual audiences and minimal ad-set structure. You supply creative and a budget, and Meta's algorithm finds buyers across Facebook and Instagram, cold and warm, the same way PMax does across Google.
- **The control that matters.** An **existing-customer budget cap**: the share of spend allowed to go to people who already bought from you. Without it, the campaign quietly retargets past buyers and books easy repeat sales as new growth.
- **Why it works.** Facebook needs about 50 purchases a week per ad set to exit learning. Splitting purchases across many ad sets starves each one.

**Worked example (1 Oct):** Facebook bought 11 purchases across 9 ad sets in 4 campaigns. That is about 77 a week across the account, but only 8.6 per ad set. One consolidated Advantage+ campaign at the same £130/day would feed all ~77 into one learning pool, which clears the 50 threshold on its own.

**Where to build it:** Ads Manager → Create → Sales → Advantage+ sales campaign → connect the catalogue → set the existing-customer cap (start at ~20%).

**Ask Daryl this:** "Why are we running four manual Facebook campaigns at £130/day instead of one Advantage+ Sales campaign with a 20% existing-customer cap, given we buy only ~75 purchases a week?" A good answer names a specific test result or a reason the CSS/[RDD] setup needs manual campaigns. An evasive answer is "Advantage+ doesn't work for us," with no data behind it.

TikTok's equivalent is **GMV Max / Smart+**: the same consolidation logic.

**You should now be able to** explain why ad-set count limits Facebook's learning, and build an Advantage+ Sales campaign with a sensible existing-customer cap.
