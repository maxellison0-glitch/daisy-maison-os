## Lesson of the Day - confidential for Max only

### Seasonality versus account performance

Seasonality means demand changes because the buying moment changes, not because the ad account suddenly got better or worse.

Yesterday is a clean example. The site did its job: 787 sessions turned into a 5.7% conversion rate, checkout abandonment stayed under the 40% flag at 33.8%, and AOV was GBP 28.92. Those are healthy operating numbers.

But sales were still only GBP 1,503.64 on GBP 552.19 spend, giving 2.72x ROAS. The reason is product demand mix. Visible teacher lines were down to about 2 order-lines / GBP 29, while visible wedding/engagement lines carried roughly 29 order-lines / GBP 445. Teacher did not disappear because the ads broke; it faded because the end-of-school buying window is nearly over.

That distinction matters. If conversion rate had dropped under 3% or checkout abandonment had jumped over 40%, the fix would be site/offer friction. But when CVR and AOV are healthy and the seasonal category is fading, the right move is budget discipline: stop expecting teacher to carry July, keep the proven wedding base stable, and get the new home products into the ad mix as soon as the listings and assets are ready.
