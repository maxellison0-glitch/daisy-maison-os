## Lesson of the Day — Ad Set Creative Rotation

When an ad set's CPP drifts upward over 3+ consecutive days — as test_creatives_2 moved from £6.80 to £13.08 — the platform has exhausted the responsive audience for that creative, and frequency is rising against a shrinking pool. The fix is not to kill the ad set or raise the budget; it's to introduce new creative INTO the winning ad set so Facebook's algorithm has fresh assets to test against the same audience. This preserves the ad set's accumulated learning data while giving it new fuel. Yesterday's numbers show NEW-HOT at £7.85 CPP is already outperforming the fatigued creative — a sign that audience targeting in TOF-WEDDING is still sound, and only the creative needs refreshing.
