## Lesson of the Day

Confidential for Max only, not for Daryl.

Today's concept: day-of-week baselines.

A day can look good or bad only against the right comparison. Tuesday is not Sunday, and a GBP 584 spend day is not a GBP 700 spend day. The useful question is: "Given this day of week and this spend level, what should the store have produced?"

Yesterday is a clean example. Spend was GBP 584.24 and sales were GBP 1,993.65, giving 3.41x ROAS. Last Tuesday, 14 Jul, spend was GBP 634.44 and total sales were GBP 2,161.50, also 3.41x ROAS. The revenue was lower yesterday because spend was lower, not because efficiency broke.

That matters for decisions. If sales fall but ROAS holds, the answer is usually "volume changed", not "the ads are broken". If sales fall and ROAS falls at the same time, then we look harder at demand quality, funnel, or campaign mix. Yesterday belongs in the first bucket: efficient Tuesday, slightly lower scale.
