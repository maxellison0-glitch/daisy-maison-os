## Lesson of the Day - Confidential for Max Only

Concept: product concentration risk.

Product concentration means one product or one small product group is carrying a big share of the day. It is not automatically bad. It is actually useful when the hero product is strong. But it changes how you read the numbers, because a "store" result can really be a "Mr & Mrs plus a few extras" result.

Yesterday is a clean example. The store did 42 orders. Mr & Mrs alone did 18 of them, which is 42.9% of all orders. The next best true product was Family Street Sign on 5 orders. Teacher products, which were the growth story last week, had faded to a handful of visible lines.

Why this matters: if ROAS is red at 2.23x, the first question is not "are all ads broken?" It is "which product demand pool carried the day, and which one disappeared?" Yesterday says wedding/Mr & Mrs still works, teacher is winding down, and social traffic volume was lighter. So the correct response is not a broad panic cut. It is to keep the wedding/PMAX base stable, accept teacher's seasonal fade, and watch whether social demand comes back today.
