## 📚 Lesson of the Day

**Device segmentation: one checkout, two platforms, three devices**

*Builds on "Checkout breaks are ad problems too".* A broken step doesn't hit every visitor equally. It usually breaks on one device.

**Segmentation** means splitting a result by one attribute, here phone/desktop/tablet. **Smart Bidding** (Google's per-auction bid) already prices each device using its recent conversion rate. So when mobile checkout breaks, PMax sees fewer mobile conversions and bids mobile down a few days later. You can't set device bid adjustments in PMax. The algorithm reacts to the damage instead of telling you about it.

**Worked example (30 Sep–6 Oct, Windsor):** Google spent £2,965.87 on mobile (92%) for £5,832.15 platform value = **1.97x**, against desktop £244.55 → £556.04 = 2.27x. Shopify mobile abandonment was 43.2% yesterday vs 35.9% for the week. The money is on mobile, and mobile checkout is where it's leaking.

**TikTok:** it's ~all mobile, so a mobile checkout fault would hit it 100%.

**Ask Daryl this:** "With 92% of Google spend on mobile and our mobile checkout worsening, do you hold PMax budget until it's fixed, or let Smart Bidding absorb it?" Good: a clear hold/cut with a date. Evasive: "the algorithm adjusts."

**You should now be able to** say whether a red day is a device-level store fault before blaming the ads.
