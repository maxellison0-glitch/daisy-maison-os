## Lesson Of The Day - Confidential For Max Only

Concept: budget pacing.

Ad platforms do not spend in a perfectly smooth line through the day. They pace budget based on auctions, expected conversion opportunity, and campaign constraints. That means yesterday's spend shape matters less than where the full day's budget landed and whether the mix matched demand.

On 14 July, spend paced into a clean balance: Google GBP 339.36 and Facebook GBP 295.08. Teacher Gifts was down to GBP 117.12, while TOF-WEDDING stayed similar at GBP 115.68 and PMAX CSS held the biggest single budget at GBP 279.37. That is good pacing because teacher is late-season and wedding/search are the steadier demand pools.

The lesson: do not judge a campaign by one moment's delivery speed. Judge the completed day by spend mix, clicks bought, and Shopify ROAS. Yesterday passed that test: GBP 634 spend, GBP 2,161.50 sales, 3.41x ROAS.
