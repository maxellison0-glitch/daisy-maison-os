## 📚 Lesson of the Day — Ad Set Naming Conventions as Diagnostic Tools

When a new ad set like "lead_images" appears in your account overnight, the naming convention tells you what changed before you open Ads Manager: "lead" likely means lead-generation creative (lifestyle/aspirational imagery designed to capture interest rather than drive purchases), while "ai_images," "product_images," and "friends_images" describe the creative type tested. Reading the naming pattern across all ad sets is the fastest diagnostic — it tells you what's being tested, what creative hypothesis is behind each ad set, and which ones share an audience (same campaign) versus which are in separate campaigns with separate budgets. This is how you audit Daryl's testing decisions without opening Ads Manager: if three ad sets in the same campaign all target the same product with different creative, the test is creative; if they're in different campaigns, the budgets are independent and can't be compared directly.
