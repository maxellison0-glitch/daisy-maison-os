## Lesson of the Day - Budget mix versus campaign panic

Confidential for Max only, not for Daryl.

A good ad account is not just about whether yesterday was green or red. It is about whether the spend is sitting in the right places for the season.

Yesterday spent GBP 620. About GBP 324 went through Google CSS/PMax and GBP 296 went through Facebook. That is almost a 52/48 Google/Facebook split. Inside Facebook, Teacher Gifts spent GBP 136.74, TOF-WEDDING spent GBP 104.01, and TEST OCT 2022 spent GBP 55.43.

The important bit: Teacher Gifts is no longer being pushed at the GBP 185-190/day level from peak teacher week. It is down to GBP 136.74. That is what a taper looks like. You are not turning it off emotionally because one day softens; you are lowering exposure as the seasonal window closes, while keeping Wedding and PMAX stable because they are the year-round gross engines.

This is the difference between campaign panic and budget mix management. Panic asks: "Was yesterday below 3x? Cut something." Budget mix asks: "Which demand curve are we in, and where should the next pound sit?" On Monday the answer was: the account produced 3.10x, teacher still sold across several SKUs, but wedding still carried higher gross. So the correct move is not a broad cut. It is a controlled teacher taper plus stable wedding/PMAX spend.
