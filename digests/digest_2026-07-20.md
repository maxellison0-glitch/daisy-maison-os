## Lesson of the Day - confidential for Max only

Concept: demand capture versus demand creation.

Google Shopping/PMax mostly captures demand that already exists. Someone searches for a wedding gift, a personalised sign, or a teacher present, and Google chooses which product card to show. That is why product titles matter so much: they help Google understand which search demand you are eligible to enter.

Facebook TOF is different. It creates or stimulates demand by putting the product in front of people who were not necessarily searching at that moment. That can still be valuable, but it is a different job. You judge it by whether the traffic it creates later turns into Shopify revenue, not just whether it buys cheap clicks.

Yesterday shows the split clearly. Search delivered 436 sessions, 46.2% of traffic, and Google spent GBP 373.11 for 588 clicks. Facebook spent GBP 257.59 and bought 695 clicks, with TOF-WEDDING alone buying 561 clicks. The store result was GBP 1,890.83 total sales and 3.00x ROAS.

The practical read: keep Google stable because it is capturing high-intent wedding demand, and keep TOF-WEDDING stable because it is feeding social discovery now teacher demand has gone. Do not confuse the two jobs. Google is the demand-capture engine; Facebook TOF is the demand-creation and audience-feeding engine. The account needs both, but right now both should be pointed at wedding/home, not teacher.
