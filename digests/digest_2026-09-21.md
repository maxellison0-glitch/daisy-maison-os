## 📚 Lesson of the Day: Minimum ROAS Bidding — When the Algorithm Says No

Christmas Static Posts uses Min ROAS bidding — the algorithm won't spend your budget unless it predicts the return will meet your floor. That's why it spent £36.37 against a £30 budget today (121%) but only £9.56 against £15 on 17 Sep (64%): the algorithm found more qualified buyers on a busier Saturday. This is the correct bid strategy for a campaign where you'd rather miss volume than waste money — the opposite of Lowest Cost, which spends everything regardless. When you run ads in-house, Min ROAS on proven seasonal campaigns and Lowest Cost on cold prospecting is the standard playbook.
