## 📚 Lesson of the Day

**Q4 CPM inflation.** This builds on CPC vs CPM. CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions) is set by an auction, so it rises when more advertisers bid for the same eyeballs. From October to December the big retailers flood Facebook and Google, and CPMs typically climb in steps toward Black Friday and early December. The same ad can cost noticeably more to show without anything in your account changing. Yesterday Christmas creative cost £7.00 CPM (£44.94 ÷ 6,418 × 1,000) against £4.82 for TOF-WEDDING (£158.86 ÷ 32,988 × 1,000), and Google PMax ran at £8.48. That is why agencies seed Christmas campaigns in September, while attention is still cheap enough for the algorithm to learn who buys. A Christmas ad set that is still in learning in late November pays peak prices to learn. TikTok's auction inflates the same way. **Ask Daryl this:** "What CPM rise are you planning for into Black Friday, and at what Christmas CPP do you stop scaling?" A good answer names a number and a date. An evasive one is "we'll see how it goes." **You should now be able to** tell whether a rising CPM in November is the market or your ad, by checking whether CTR held.
