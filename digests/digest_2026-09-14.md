---

## Lesson of the Day

### Portfolio Drag and the Denominator Problem

When you add a campaign to the account, its spend goes into the ROAS denominator whether it converts or not. Today's diffuser campaigns spent £167.49 on 4 purchases; stripping them out lifts ROAS from 2.00x to 2.47x on the same revenue. The decision isn't "is this campaign doing something" — it's "is it earning its share of the denominator." Every pound a bad campaign spends makes every good campaign's contribution look worse in the blended number.
