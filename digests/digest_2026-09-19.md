## 📚 Lesson of the Day

**First-Party Data & Customer Lists**

Every Shopify order gives you an email address. Both Facebook and Google let you upload those emails as a Custom Audience — the platform matches them to real user accounts and lets you target (or exclude) those exact people. This is first-party data: information your customers gave you directly, which no privacy change or iOS update can take away. The operational use is three-fold: build a lookalike from your best buyers (higher quality than pixel-only lookalikes), exclude existing customers from cold campaigns to stop paying twice, and create a retention audience for repeat-purchase campaigns. In Google Ads it's Customer Match (Tools → Audience Manager → Customer Lists); in Facebook it's Custom Audiences → Customer List. Ask Daryl whether a Shopify customer list has been uploaded to either platform, and if so, when it was last refreshed — a list older than 90 days is decaying.
