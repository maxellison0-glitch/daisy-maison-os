## Confidential Lesson of the Day - Max Only

Concept: contribution after ad spend.

ROAS tells you how much revenue came back for each £1 spent on ads. Contribution after ad spend asks the next question: how much revenue is left before product cost, labour, fees, and overheads?

Yesterday Daisy spent £541.92 and took £1,654.51 total sales. That is 3.05x ROAS, so the store had £1,112.59 of revenue left after ad spend. That does not mean £1,112.59 profit, because product costs, shipping materials, payment fees, Daryl, labour, and overhead still come out afterwards.

This is why a 3.05x day is green but not wildly comfortable. It clears the ad hurdle, and the £30.08 AOV helps, but the next quality question is whether the sales mix carries enough margin after ads. Add-ons help here because Large upgrades, Medium upgrades, Mounting Strips, Gift Wrap, and Easel added visible high-margin revenue to orders that were already acquired.

The practical rule: use ROAS to decide if paid traffic is broadly acceptable, then use contribution after ad spend and product mix to decide whether the day was actually attractive.
