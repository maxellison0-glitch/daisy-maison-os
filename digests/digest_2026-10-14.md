## 📚 Lesson of the Day *(confidential: Max only, not Daryl)*

**Graduating a winning creative: Post ID and social proof**

*This builds on ABO vs CBO and significant edits.* A **test ad set** is a small box where new ads compete. When one wins, you move it into the **main ad set** (the steady earner). The obvious way is to duplicate the ad. That's wrong: a duplicate is a brand-new post with zero likes, comments and shares, and that **social proof** (visible engagement that makes strangers trust an ad) is part of why it won. The right way is **"Use existing post"** with the winner's **Post ID** (the number that identifies the post itself). The same post, with its comments, then runs in both places. **Failure looks like:** the "winner" moves over and its CPP doubles. The new ad also triggers learning in the main ad set, so move winners in batches, not one a day.

**Worked example:** test_creatives got 7 purchases for £27.39 = **£3.91 CPP**. TOF - MAIN got 1 for £15.70. That's one day and 7 sales, too few to call. Graduate after ~3 days and 15+ purchases.

**Ask Daryl this:** "When a test creative wins, do you move it into MAIN by Post ID or by duplicating?" Good answer: "Post ID, batched weekly." Evasive: "Facebook handles it."

**TikTok:** Spark Ads do the same job; they run an existing post through an authorisation code.

**You should now be able to** move a winning ad without throwing away the engagement that made it win.
