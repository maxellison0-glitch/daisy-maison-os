## 📚 Lesson of the Day — Meta Business Suite & Ad Account Access Levels

**Callback:** On 25 Aug you learned about Google Ads Change History — the log that shows who changed what. Today's lesson is the other side: how to get into the account in the first place, and specifically why you cannot just "log in" to the Daisy Maison Facebook ads.

### What Meta Business Suite Actually Is

Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com) is a wrapper that sits between your personal Facebook account and every business asset you manage — ad accounts, Pages, Instagram accounts, pixels, product catalogues. Think of it as an access-control layer: **your personal Facebook login is the key, but Business Suite decides which doors that key opens.**

The hierarchy is:

```
Business Manager (one per business, e.g. "Daisy Maison")
├── Ad Account (e.g. 1574764016252349 — this is the one Windsor reads)
│   ├── Campaigns (TOF-WEDDING, TEST OCT 2022, Social Engagement)
│   │   ├── Ad Sets (Copy, more, New Sales)
│   │   │   └── Ads (the actual creative)
├── Facebook Page (Daisy Maison UK)
├── Instagram Account (@daisymaisonuk)
├── Pixel (the tracking code on your Shopify store)
└── Product Catalogue (your Shopify feed for dynamic ads)
```

Each asset has its own access level, and each person added to the Business Manager gets a **role** that determines what they can see and do.

### The Three Ad Account Roles

1. **Admin** — full control: create/edit/pause campaigns, change budgets, add/remove people, view billing, export data. This is what Daryl has.
2. **Advertiser** — can create and edit campaigns, change budgets, view reporting. Cannot manage people or billing. **This is what you need.**
3. **Analyst** — read-only. Can view campaigns and reporting but cannot change anything. Useful for bookkeepers, not operators.

The critical detail: **Windsor.ai connects at the Analyst level.** It reads data — spend, clicks, impressions, campaign structure — but it cannot change anything. That's why you can see everything in the morning digest but cannot pause TEST OCT 2022, enforce the TOF-WEDDING spend cap, or shift budget between ad sets. **You have a window into the ad account. You need a door.**

### How Access Gets Granted

There are exactly two paths:

**Path 1 — Daryl adds you (fastest).** In Business Suite: Settings → People → Add People → enter your email → assign "Advertiser" on the ad account. Takes 30 seconds. You accept an email invitation, and you're in.

**Path 2 — You request access (if Daryl won't respond).** In your own Business Suite (create one at business.facebook.com if you don't have one): Settings → Requests → Request Access to an Ad Account → enter the ad account ID `1574764016252349`. Daryl (or whoever is Admin) sees the request and approves it. This is slower but doesn't require Daryl to initiate.

The arithmetic: 604 + 59 = 663 Facebook clicks yesterday, all cold or engagement. At a ~2% cold-to-warm conversion rate, ~13 of those visitors entered the pixel's warm audience pool. With TEST OCT 2022 running, those 13 people would have seen retargeting ads over the next 3–7 days, converting at an estimated 8–12% (vs ~2% cold). That's ~1–2 additional orders per day at zero incremental spend, worth roughly £30–60/day in revenue.

Over 13 days: ~£400–780 in directly attributable lost revenue from the retargeting gap alone. The broader effect is worse — the warm audience pool built by 23 days of TOF-WEDDING spending (£4,800+ total) is decaying without being monetised.

**The cost of not having access is not theoretical. It is £180+/day and compounding.**
