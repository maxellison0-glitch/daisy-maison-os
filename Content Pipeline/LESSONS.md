# Lessons from published posts

Split out of `PUBLISH_LOG.md` on 29 Jul 2026. That file is a **record** — dates,
platforms, numbers. This one holds the reasoning, which was making rows
thousands of characters long and unreadable as a table.

Nothing here is new. Every entry is preserved from the log rows it came from.

---

## DM-C018-BOND — Instagram, 25 Jul 2026

First real post on the new system. The audience did not read it as AI.

**Then it was corrected by measurement, and that is the lesson.** It was logged
as our best-engaging post on Max's recollection, and a whole strategy argument
was built on it — back the video lane, one video a day on Instagram. Measured
28 Jul: **7 likes, 0 comments**, against 6 for the cat reel and 4 plus 1 comment
for the diffuser carousel. A three-like spread is noise.

We spent three weeks reasoning from a remembered number, and one unauthenticated
HTTP request would have settled it on day one.

## DM-CAT-HOUSE — Instagram, 26 Jul 2026

The asset that shipped was `CAT-ADVERT-master-v2.mp4`, the **original master**.
A locally regraded wall with the string painted out was offered first and Max
rejected it outright: *"that video looks ridiculous. The string is not
consistent. It's like a literal video effects glitch... the rendering you did
looks awful."*

Two lessons: a hand-built pixel pipeline on moving footage shows every seam, and
it cost him his whole midday slot. **The finished master was already in the repo
the entire time.**

## DM-DIFFUSER-TEASE — TikTok + Instagram, 27 Jul 2026

**First post this system published itself.** 3-slide 4:5 photo carousel, hooks
on option A after Max rejected the first set as AI slop. Route: local build
(0 credits) → Higgsfield media host → TikTok Content Posting API.

The product is **not launched**, so the copy teased "live this week" and made no
price, scent or delivery claim. Choices were Max's: music on, commercial-content
disclosure OFF, comments ON, AIGC disclosure ON (the stills are synthetic).

**Open failure, found 29 Jul:** there is still no diffuser SKU in the Shopify
catalogue. The 27 Jul copy promised "live this week" and it is now Wednesday
with nothing to sell. Do not tease a product that does not exist as a purchasable
thing.

## DM-HOUSE-QUIZ — TikTok 27 Jul, Instagram 29 Jul

**First post built on the Freya character lock.** 5-slide photo carousel, one
archetype per slide, all five real colourways.

The mechanic is the point: a number is the cheapest comment there is, and
comments are TikTok's strongest reach lever, so every slide carries its number
and slide 1 carries the ask. Validated externally — TreatBox run a "Find My
Perfect Gift" quiz badged "TAKES 1 MIN" as permanent homepage furniture, so the
format is not a novelty.

Two production notes: **'THE ZOO' was refused by the generator's safety filter
on the wording alone**, swapped to THE MADHOUSE; and slide 4 carries small
gibberish printing on envelopes despite the no-printed-text room rule, so that
rule needs enforcing **per-object, not per-room**.

## DM-C017-JANNAWAY — TikTok, 28 Jul 2026

**The PII gate closed, by Max's explicit decision.** This reel had been held
since 23 Jul because the sign carries a real customer's surname **and** their
wedding date, 15 Aug 2026 — three weeks after posting. Surname alone is product
wording and has always been fine; surname plus an exact future date identifies a
specific couple and publishes their date before it happens, which is a different
thing.

Four routes were offered, including posting Bond instead at zero cost and zero
question. Max chose to post Jannaway as is, confirming the couple are content.

Recorded because the reasoning matters more than the outcome: **the gate was
real, it was raised, and it was cleared by the person entitled to clear it.**

PILL hook style, 8.0s, silent master. AIGC disclosure ON — the presenter is
synthetic, which is the strongest disclosure case there is.
