# Daisy Maison Idea Bank

Ideas belong here only when they can become a specific post. Move selected
ideas into `PRODUCTION_QUEUE.md`; do not turn this into a general notes dump.

## Repeatable series

### Personalisation reveals

- Blank heart to finished named wedding heart in 15 seconds.
- Website customiser to artwork to the parcel leaving the workshop.
- Hide the finished names, show the making, then reveal the full design.
- "The moment this stops being a product and becomes their gift."

### Choices that earn comments

- One heart or two: which would you give the couple?
- Three wedding gift-box messages: pick 1, 2, or 3.
- Small, medium, or large Mr & Mrs sign shown honestly in hand and in a room.
- Gift box, wooden easel, or mounting strips: which presentation wins?
- Two name layouts for the same couple: A or B?

### Gift-problem solvers

- What do you buy a couple who already has everything?
- Three personalised wedding gifts that do not feel generic.
- The ordering mistakes that cause personalised-gift panic: spelling, size,
  date, and leaving it too late.
- A wedding-anniversary idea by year: silver, ruby, golden.
- A meaningful gift when you know the couple well but not their home style.

### Workshop and trust

- Pack one real anonymised order from print to parcel.
- The quality check before a personalised sign is allowed to leave.
- How Daisy protects a pebble picture in transit.
- A busy-order table, followed by the finished parcels.
- What happens after someone presses Buy on a personalised order.
- A small error caught before dispatch and why checking matters.

### Product detail and proof

- Why the Mr & Mrs street sign keeps becoming the bestseller.
- Close-up texture and legibility test: what it actually looks like, not a mockup.
- One product styled three ways: shelf, wall, and gift-box reveal.
- The difference an easel or gift box makes to the same gift.
- An anonymised order of the day and the story the chosen wording suggests.

### Seasonal rotation

- Weddings and anniversaries remain the evergreen anchor.
- Teacher content is used only while the buying window is genuinely open.
- Six to eight weeks before each major occasion, replace the optional weekly
  slot with a timely idea and a verified ordering deadline.
- Build future banks for Christmas, Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, Father's
  Day, christenings, graduations, new homes, and remembrance when relevant.

## First recommended batch

1. **Transformation:** "This is the exact moment a blank heart becomes their
   wedding gift." Real build and reveal.
2. **Choice:** "You are giving this to the couple. Which box message are you
   choosing: 1, 2, or 3?" Carousel or short video.
3. **Trust:** "What actually happens after you personalise an order with us."
   Customiser-to-parcel mini story.
4. **Gift problem:** "What do you buy the couple who already owns everything?"
   Three genuinely different Daisy options.
5. **Optional product truth:** Honest in-hand comparison of small, medium, and
   large Mr & Mrs signs.

## Hook patterns

- "You are buying for [recipient]. Which one are you choosing?"
- "Most people only see the finished gift. This is the bit before it."
- "A generic wedding gift would have been easier. Then we added their names."
- "Before you order a personalised gift, check these three things."
- "This order started as two names on a screen."
- "The couple already has everything—so what do you give them?"

Use these as structures, not permanent scripts. The footage and occasion must
earn the hook.
