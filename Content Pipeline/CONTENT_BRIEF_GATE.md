# The Brief Gate — decide *why* before we make *anything*

**Rule: no asset gets produced until this brief is filled in. Especially video.**

We were making videos for no reason — three static shots cut together because
we *could*, not because a decision said we should. That stops here. The brief
comes first, the caption is written before the visual, and video has to earn
its place over a still.

---

## Production constraints (HARD — read before routing anything)

**1. Max does not film. Ever.** "Real film", "2-minute shoot", "Max turns the
sign to camera", "grab a 3-second clip" are **not available routes** and must
never appear in a brief, an idea, or a cost estimate. Costing something at
"0 credits because we film it" is not a cheap route — it's a dead one. Any idea
whose payoff depends on new footage is dead on arrival; re-route it or drop it.

**2. Everything comes from generated stills or assets already on disk.** The
proven pipeline is personalised product stills (four completed batches) plus our
own caption/motion work in post. That is the raw material. Work with it.

**3. There is no presenter.** No Max-to-camera, no Freya-to-camera. Content is
product-led: the object, the wording, the reveal, the caption.

**4. Motion is added, never captured.** Either eased sub-pixel moves on stills
(free, local) or image-to-video on a budget model (4–8 credits, Max's approval).
Only the white wedding-sign turn family is validated for synthetic motion
(DM-C017, 8.5/10 realism) — no other sign family is proven, so don't assume one.

**5. A still or carousel is usually the right answer.** With no filming and no
presenter, a well-typeset still often beats a contrived animation. For joke-copy
signs the *line of copy is the product* — it needs legibility, not cinematics.

---

## The one-page brief (fill before producing)

1. **Objective** — what is this piece *for*? (awareness / launch / conversion /
   proof / restock nudge). One sentence. If you can't name it, don't make it.
2. **Platform & placement** — Instagram Reel? Feed carousel? Story? TikTok?
   Each wants a different shape. Pick one primary.
3. **Audience & moment** — who's scrolling, and what are they feeling when this
   hits them? (e.g. "someone hunting for a personalised gift, 2 weeks before a
   birthday, doesn't want tat.")
4. **The one idea** — the single thing they should take away. Not three.
5. **The hook / caption — WRITTEN FIRST.** The line that stops the scroll goes
   here *before* any visual exists. If the caption isn't good on its own, the
   video won't save it. (Runs through Freya & Alan's taste gate.)
6. **Format & why-not-a-still** — a still or carousel is the default (proven,
   ~free). Video must justify itself: *what does motion add that a photo can't?*
   If the honest answer is "nothing," make the still.
7. **Shot / slide list** — exactly what's on screen. For video, prefer **one
   continuous shot**; a cut has to be a deliberate idea, not a filler.
8. **Success signal** — how we'll know it worked (saves, shares, DMs, link taps).
   Vanity views don't count.
9. **Budget** — credits estimate (see `VIDEO_MODEL_COSTS.md`). Approve before spend.

---

## When video is actually worth it

Make a video **only** when at least one is true:
- Motion *is* the story — a reveal, a hand lifting the lid, flame flicker,
  ribbon pulled. Something a still genuinely can't hold.
- The platform strongly rewards it for this objective (e.g. Reels reach).
- We have a decided hook that lands better spoken/paced than static.

Otherwise: **still or carousel.** Our best work so far has been stills.

## How we build video (so it doesn't read as AI slop)

- **Caption is our overlay layer, never the model's.** We type-set it in post
  (Fraunces / editorial, tonal, placed in negative space off the product).
  We never pay a video model to burn in text.
- **One continuous ambient shot** beats three static shots hard-cut.
- **Budget model for motion** (4–8 credits) is fine when identity holds; only
  reach for premium Seedance 2.0 when the label/product must stay locked.
- **QC every frame** against the AI-slop checklist in
  `Personas/VOICE_AND_CAPTION_GUIDE.md` (vibration, morph, fake zoom = reject).
- **PII gate** — no legible real customer names/handles in anything published.
