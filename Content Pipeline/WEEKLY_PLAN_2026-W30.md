## Per-post detail

### 1 — Reed diffuser launch (hero of the week)
- **Why it works:** a sign brand launching a scent is a pattern interrupt, and the logic lands on the *existing* audience — you already sold them the personalised wall; the diffuser personalises the room. That's a cross-sell to a warm base, not a cold new category.
- **Reveal logic:** hook sets up "personalisation beyond the sign" → reveal is the name on the bottle + the scent.
- **Alt hooks:** *"POV: you personalised the whole house"* / *"The gift that makes a house smell like *them*."*
- **Assets I need from you:** one clear product photo (or the physical item to shoot), the exact **name/scent/price**, and whether it's gift-boxed. With those I can cut a launch reel (real footage) or generate a clean product shot via the IG prompt.
- **Add-on angle:** flag it as a checkout pair with home/family signs (mirrors the mounting-strips attach logic — home fragrance ↔ home sign).

### 2 — Home-bar LAUGH (real film)

- Job: shares + tags. CTA soft: *"Tag the mate."*

### 3 — Mr & Mrs Bond (already produced)

### 5 — A-or-B choice
