CHARACTER LOCKS — how Freya and Alan stay the same people
Established 27 Jul 2026. Read this before generating anyone holding a sign.

THE IDEA
--------
One approved photograph is the LOCK. Everything else - wording, colourway, room -
is prompted against it. Max, 27 Jul: "this is freyas new image sign lock for
prompting... built off daisy-maison literally making them real characters."

Tested that day and it holds. Off one Freya lock, in single takes with no
retries: three colourways, three wordings and a completely new room, with her
face and the sign-to-shoulder scale intact every time.

THE THREE BLOCKS EVERY PROMPT NEEDS
-----------------------------------
1. KEEP AS REFERENCE - name the person's fixed features explicitly (face, hair,
   clothing, expression, pose). Naming them beats saying "same person".
2. THE PRODUCT - 570mm x 125mm, shaped notched ends, cream panel, coloured
   printed border, real edge thickness, NO mounting holes.

   RESOLVED 28 Jul 2026, by Max: "We're not doing holes, ideally. That is
   going forward: don't do that." NO MOUNTING HOLES is the rule, and it is
   not a stylistic preference to be traded away when a reference disagrees.

   The catch: Alan's lock and the real product-reference pack BOTH show two
   holes, because the real photographed signs have them. So the holes will
   arrive by inheritance unless a prompt says otherwise. **Write "no mounting
   holes, no drilled holes, unbroken panel" into the product block of every
   prompt that leans on those references**, and check the output for them
   before anything else - they are small, and they survive a thumbnail.
3. THE SCALE - held horizontally in both hands at chest height, hands wrapping
   the shaped ends, about 1.4x the holder's shoulder width. This is the gate
   from VERDICTS.md and it is the single thing that decides whether the sign
   reads as a real object or a plaque.

Then the phone-snapshot block from HOUSE-STANDARD-phone-snapshot.txt, and the
line-2 size clamp from the same file.

TWO FAULTS FOUND ON DAY ONE
---------------------------
BOOKS. Ask for a shelf of books and the spines come back as gibberish lettering.
It survives a thumbnail and it does not survive a screenshot. Write NO BOOKS AND
NO PRINTED TEXT ANYWHERE IN THE ROOM into the room brief.

TWO SIGNS IN ONE FRAME. The first two-hander take pushed both signs off the
frame edges and squashed Alan's line 2 up against line 1. The second take was
clean with no prompt change, so generate two takes whenever there are two signs
and pick - the failure mode is layout, and layout is where the variance lives.
