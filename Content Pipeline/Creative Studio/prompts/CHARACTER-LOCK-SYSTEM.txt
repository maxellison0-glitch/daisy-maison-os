CHARACTER LOCKS — how Freya and Alan stay the same people
Established 27 Jul 2026. Read this before generating anyone holding a sign.

THE IDEA
--------
One approved photograph is the LOCK. Everything else - wording, colourway, room -
is prompted against it. Max, 27 Jul: "this is freyas new image sign lock for
prompting... built off daisy-maison literally making them real characters."

Tested that day and it holds. Off one Freya lock, in single takes with no
retries: three colourways, three wordings and a completely new room, with her
face and the sign-to-shoulder scale intact every time.

THE THREE BLOCKS EVERY PROMPT NEEDS
-----------------------------------
1. KEEP AS REFERENCE - name the person's fixed features explicitly (face, hair,
   clothing, expression, pose). Naming them beats saying "same person".
2. THE PRODUCT - 570mm x 125mm, shaped notched ends, cream panel, coloured
   printed border, real edge thickness, NO mounting holes.

   RESOLVED 28 Jul 2026, by Max: "We're not doing holes, ideally. That is
   going forward: don't do that." NO MOUNTING HOLES is the rule, and it is
   not a stylistic preference to be traded away when a reference disagrees.

   The catch: Alan's lock and the real product-reference pack BOTH show two
   holes, because the real photographed signs have them. So the holes will
   arrive by inheritance unless a prompt says otherwise. **Write "no mounting
   holes, no drilled holes, unbroken panel" into the product block of every
   prompt that leans on those references**, and check the output for them
   before anything else - they are small, and they survive a thumbnail.

   PROVEN 28 Jul 2026, AND THE SENTENCE IS NOT ENOUGH. Sixteen takes were
   generated across four batches (DM-C020 cast run), every single prompt
   carrying "NO MOUNTING HOLES ANYWHERE - the panel is unbroken":

     batch    lock used                    holes in output
     cast-A   Alan   (lock HAS holes)      YES
     cast-B   Alan   (lock HAS holes)      YES
     cast-C   Freya  (lock has NONE)       NO
     cast-D   Alan + Freya                 YES, on Alan's sign only

   That is a clean natural experiment with one variable. A NEGATIVE INSTRUCTION
   DOES NOT BEAT A REFERENCE IMAGE. The model is copying the holes out of the
   photograph, and no amount of rewording the prompt will stop it.

   The fix is to change the reference, not the sentence: produce a hole-free
   Alan lock once - the approved black-tee lock with the two holes patched -
   and re-approve it. Every future generation then inherits a clean panel.
   Changing a lock needs Max, which is why this is written down and not done.
   Until then, plan on removing them in post on any Alan output.

   The general rule this establishes, worth more than the holes themselves:
   when a prompt instruction and a reference image disagree, THE REFERENCE
   WINS. Fix the reference.
3. THE SCALE.

   *** THE GATE, REPLACED 28 Jul 2026 BY MAX. READ THIS ONE, NOT THE RATIO. ***

   THE SIGN'S ENDS MUST NOT EXTEND PAST THE OUTER EDGE OF HIS ARMS.

   Max, 28 Jul, reviewing eleven in-office images: "On Dave's Bar and Grill
   image 1, 3, and 4, all extend past his shoulder line on the right or near
   his shoulder line. On image 2, its hand is in the same placement, if
   anything closer, and the sign does not reach past him. On image 2, that
   looks way more realistic."

   The reference image for correct scale is
     active/DM-C020-office-remodel/working/ideas-B-bargrill/outputs/take-02.png
   Its ends land AT his arms and his hands are not stretched out wide. That is
   the standard. Check every output against it before anything else.

   HOW TO CHECK IT: draw a vertical line down the frame at each end of the sign.
   If either line falls outside his arm, the sign is too big. It takes seconds
   and it does not need a detector.

   WHAT THE OLD GATE SAID, AND WHY IT IS NOT THE GATE ANY MORE. The previous
   rule was "about 1.4x the holder's shoulder width", later measured at ~1.35
   off real footage. The approved image above FAILS that ratio and is the most
   realistic of the set; three images that PASS it were rejected on sight as
   too big. The ratio is kept below as background only. Perspective is why:
   how far the sign is in front of the body changes its apparent width far
   more than its real width does, so a body-relative ratio measures the wrong
   thing. The arm line is a silhouette test and perspective cannot cheat it.

   Background, no longer the gate: held horizontally in both hands at chest
   height, hands wrapping the shaped ends, roughly 1.3-1.4x shoulder width
   measured at the sleeve seam in real footage.

   GROUND TRUTH, measured 28 Jul 2026 off real footage of Max holding a real
   sign in the real unit (instagram-DPjdseCDbDR, the 11,889-view post): sign
   ~975px, shoulders at the SLEEVE SEAM ~720px, ratio ~1.35. The 1.20-1.45
   window is right.

   MEASURE IT AT THE SLEEVE SEAM. "Shoulder width" is four different numbers
   depending on where you measure - sleeve seam, upper arm, or elbow, and when
   the arms are out holding a sign the elbow span is far wider than the
   shoulders. Four automatic detectors written against the same sixteen images
   returned four different verdicts for exactly this reason. Until a detector
   anchored to the sleeve seam exists, this gate is a by-eye judgement and
   should be called one.

   AND WRITING THE NUMBER IN THE PROMPT DOES NOT ENFORCE IT. "The sign is 1.30
   times the width of the man's shoulders" was in every DM-C020 cast prompt and
   the scale still varied from about 1.1 to well over 1.5 across takes. Generate
   several and pick; do not expect the sentence to do the work.

TWO FAULTS FOUND ON DAY ONE
---------------------------
BOOKS. Ask for a shelf of books and the spines come back as gibberish lettering.
It survives a thumbnail and it does not survive a screenshot. Write NO BOOKS AND
NO PRINTED TEXT ANYWHERE IN THE ROOM into the room brief.

TWO SIGNS IN ONE FRAME. The first two-hander take pushed both signs off the
frame edges and squashed Alan's line 2 up against line 1. The second take was
clean with no prompt change, so generate two takes whenever there are two signs
and pick - the failure mode is layout, and layout is where the variance lives.
