## Generative production memory

- Max is the final taste and global product-realism gate. `agent-pass` is never
  equivalent to `max-approved`.
- Log every generation immediately in the active concept: prompt, model,
  references, job/media IDs, output, decision and reusable failure lesson.
- Promote only Max-approved recipes into `recipes/`.
- Preserve exact dimensions, construction and scale cues alongside wording and
  artwork; never hyperfocus on one invariant and ignore the whole object.
- Never overlay, corner-pin, track, redraw or replace a sign face in final
  video. This includes whole-panel and lettering-only replacements. The exact
  sign must survive natively from the approved source state; otherwise reject
  and regenerate.
- Never send a composited sign still into a video model. A human must first
  approve a single coherent generated image containing the exact SVG as a real
  sign.
- No video spend without real front, two opposing edge/three-quarter and back
  references. The edges lock the thin pale/white physical profile; the reverse
  locks the real white back. The black border belongs to the front artwork,
  not a thick black slab.
- A border colour seam, a whiter replacement panel, mismatched reflections,
  blur, edge thickness, contact shadow or temporal lighting is an automatic
  human-visual rejection. Exact pixels or automated lettering QC cannot
  overrule a product that looks pasted on.
- Use Codex or Claude Opus for archaeology and forensic review. Reserve Fable 5
  for future-facing concept and design work unless Max says otherwise.
