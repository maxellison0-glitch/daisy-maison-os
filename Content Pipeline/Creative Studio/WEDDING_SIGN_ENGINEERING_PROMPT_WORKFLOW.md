## Validated scope

This method is validated only for the demonstrated white wedding-sign family:

- white front and plain-white reverse;
- broad black printed front border;
- black Times New Roman wedding wording;
- red heart;
- the audited Large 570 × 125 mm construction and proportions;
- surname/date-led wedding or newlywed creative.

It is **not** yet a validated general street-sign method. The following remain
separate, unfinished engineering lanes:

- coloured sign faces with black text;
- coloured sign faces with coloured text;
- any different font, border system, material response or construction;
- non-wedding sign categories.

Do not promote those variants by analogy. Each needs its own approved real
product authority, exact artwork test, successful still sequence, native video
proof and human realism approval before it can be described as working.

## Non-negotiable boundary

The sign must be physically coherent before video generation and must survive
the native video.

Never apply a whole-panel, border, lettering-only, tracked, corner-pinned,
homography, masked, inpainted or 3D-texture replacement to the moving sign.

This rule exists because humans see differences in white balance, material
grain, reflections, blur, border tone, contact shadows, edge depth and finger
occlusion immediately. A mathematically exact graphic can still look like a
sticker. DM-C017 v02 proved that once a replacement face is visible, the
advert loses trust.

## Stage 1 — choose display-safe demand

1. Read Shopify orders and line items only as required and only read-only.
2. Select wording supported by real demand, or label an invented idea honestly.
3. Names and the intended personalised sign wording are usable display
   content. Never carry addresses, contact, payment or unrelated customer
   information into production.
4. Record whether the exact wording is real incoming artwork or a safe
   invention.

DM-C017 used the real incoming `MR & MRS [SURNAME]` pattern and a date, which
dominated the audited order sample.

## Stage 2 — build exact artwork first

1. Put the wording into the audited LightBurn-derived Large sign system.
2. Produce the exact SVG, transparent PNG and 570 × 125 mm visual PDF.
3. Lock the wording, Times New Roman treatment, red heart, holes, border,
   contour and 4.56:1 proportion before asking an image model to make it real.
4. The artwork owns printing only. It does not own material, thickness,
   lighting or hand contact.

## Stage 3 — create the real-looking product image

Give Nano Banana 2:

1. one real Daisy Maison sign photograph as the locked manufactured-object
   authority; and
2. the exact LightBurn-derived sign face as replacement printing only.

The literal operation is: keep the real sign and photograph unchanged; replace
only the printing already on its front.

The real photograph owns:

- silhouette and shaped ends;
- broad printed border and holes;
- rigid material and shallow pale construction;
- perspective, surface response, hands, occlusion and shadows;
- exposure, focus, grain and lens character.

The exact artwork owns:

- wording and every letter form;
- heart;
- line placement, spacing and relative scale;
- print colours.

Generate four to eight inexpensive identical-prompt takes. Do not add Max,
motion or other variables yet. A human must approve one untouched coherent
product image.

## Stage 6 — assemble the video reference contract

Reference roles must be explicit and non-overlapping.

| Input | Sole authority |
| --- | --- |
| Start image | First frame, synthetic Max, camera, plain-white reverse |
| End image | Final frame, synthetic Max, camera, exact finished face |
| Approved product image | Front print, material, border, proportions |
| Real front-to-edge crop | Thin pale edge and front-to-edge relationship |
| Real back-to-edge crop | Thin pale edge and back-to-edge relationship |
| Real white-back crop | Reverse and mounting-hole truth |
| Real motion crop | Hand choreography and perspective change only |

When a real Reel supplies motion, crop out the source performer's face,
identity, captions and readable alternative front. DM-C017 retained frames
60–180 and pixels `y=760..1499`: white back through the thin edge only. It did
not supply identity or replacement wording.

## Stage 7 — buy one native video, not a batch

Use Seedance 2.0 through Higgsfield's bundled native `vendor\hf.exe`. The
Windows `.cmd` wrapper previously truncated long prompts and lost references.

Required sequence:

1. inspect the model schema;
2. quote the exact cost;
3. show the quote before submission;
4. submit one job;
5. inspect the returned job JSON to prove every parameter and media role
   arrived;
6. download the untouched native result;
7. do not buy a retry until the first result is judged.

Validated settings:

- Seedance 2.0;
- standard mode;
- 1080p, high bitrate;
- 9:16;
- eight seconds;
- generated audio off;
- multi-shot off;
- approved start and end images;
- four construction/product image references;
- one privacy-cropped real motion reference;
- 72 credits.

The prompt locks:

- exactly one continuous take;
- one monotonic 180° vertical-axis yaw;
- no cut, camera move or speed-ramp edit;
- continuous hands and physically supported wrists/arms;
- thin rigid plaque, white reverse and front-only black border;
- exact final wording and at least 2.7 seconds readable hold.

## Stage 8 — untouched-native QC

Decode every frame before adding hook, CTA or sound.

Reject the complete native video if any frame shows:

- identity drift;
- failed hands or broken grip continuity;
- bent, swollen or changing sign shape;
- thick or black physical edge;
- black or decorated reverse;
- changed letter, heart, colour, border or proportion;
- non-monotonic motion, teleport, occlusion pop or hidden cut;
- product too small or unreadable.

DM-C017 native evidence:

- 1080 × 1920;
- 24 fps;
- 193 decoded frames;
- 8.0417 seconds;
- no frame-difference cut spike;
- exact endpoints;
- front readable from frame 112 through frame 192;
- 81 readable frames compared with the approved source;
- 3.375-second readable hold;
- no product-surface compositing;
- human realism score 8.5/10.

Automated checks support the decision. They never overrule a human who says
the object looks pasted on or physically wrong.

## Stage 9 — finish without touching the product

Allowed:

- hook in the top safe area;
- CTA in the bottom safe area;
- whole-shot colour treatment;
- original or licensed sound;
- exact timing, H.264/AAC encode and delivery scaling.

DM-C017 finishing:

- hook: `THEIR NEW SURNAME / DESERVES A STREET SIGN`, 0.00–2.80 seconds;
- CTA: `PERSONALISE THEIRS →`, 5.15–8.00 seconds;
- original synthetic room, turn-whoosh and reveal sound;
- first 192 native frames retained for exactly 8.000 seconds;
- product region untouched except normal H.264 re-encoding;
- 1080 × 1920 master and 720 × 1280 preview.

Human approval remains mandatory at:

1. coherent product image;
2. synthetic-Max hero;

approved still selections change. The construction pack, reference roles,

Coloured or non-wedding products must not enter this workflow until separately
validated.
