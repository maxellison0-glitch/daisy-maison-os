# The Morning Of - production treatment

## Method lock

The product is never pinned over a separately moving video. Each shot begins as
one approved, coherent 9:16 keyframe with the product already integrated using
the Shopify originals as Higgsfield product references. Higgsfield then animates
the complete frame. Post-production only cuts shots, grades, mixes sound and adds
editorial typography.

The twelve-second film is not one long generation. It is four controlled short
generations joined by motivated physical cuts.

## 0.0-2.5 - The handover

A mother slides the closed `From this day forward` box across a sunlit bridal
dressing table into her daughter's hands. Bride wears an ivory morning robe.
Genuine hand, sleeve and box movement begins immediately.

Reference: `assets/04-box-from-this-day-forward.png`.

## 2.5-4.5 - The first look

Close continuation. The bride's fingertips settle on the lid and lift it only
one centimetre. Cut during the rising action; do not generate the entire opening
or expose the heart under moving occlusion.

References: `assets/04-box-from-this-day-forward.png` plus the approved Shot 1
character/room keyframe.

## 4.5-7.5 - The truthful reveal

Hard action cut to a matched-angle open-box keyframe. The exact heart rests in
the box; the ribbon responds subtly as fingertips enter and lift only its loose
end. The heart itself remains still and readable.

References: `assets/02-gift-box-heart.png` and
`assets/01-main-open-heart.png`.

## 7.5-12.0 - Beside the dress

Match the lifted ribbon into the heart already hanging beside the waiting ivory
wedding dress. Quiet window light, a curtain breathes, and the complete scene
moves as one Higgsfield-generated shot. The heart makes one very small physical
swing and settles.

The dress/heart keyframe is created and approved before video generation, with
`assets/01-main-open-heart.png` as the canonical product reference. There is no
tracked product overlay.

End copy: `Made personal for their wedding day` then `Daisy Maison`.

Canonical product: the `Dan & Rebecca` heart shown in assets 01 and 05. Asset 02
may guide the open-box composition, but its `Mr & Mrs Owens 26/07/2026` wording
must not appear legibly or be blended into the canonical heart.

## Sound

Morning room tone, box on fabric, fingertips on textured lid, lid movement,
ribbon and curtain. No electronic cue, piano sting or voiceover in the first
cut.

## Production stages

1. Approve a four-frame Popcorn storyboard sharing one bridal-suite look.
2. Refine four final 9:16 keyframes; reject product or lettering drift now.
3. Generate three short takes per shot at economical preview quality.
4. Select motion winners, then regenerate/upscale only the approved takes.
5. Edit, mix, grade and add safe-zone typography after picture lock.

## Automatic rejection

- any floating or sliding product layer;
- altered pebble count, hat, veil, pink heart, names or date;
- malformed hands or fabric passing through the box;
- complete AI-generated box opening;
- a shot whose only movement is a crop or zoom;
- square-image padding or low-resolution punch-ins;
- inconsistent bride, robe, room or window direction;
- illegible product on a phone.
