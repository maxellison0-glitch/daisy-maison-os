# DM-C017 V03 eight-second creative structure

Status: **implemented and verified in the finished v03 Reel.**

This solves the earlier logic failure: a technically smooth turn is not a Reel
unless the viewer immediately understands why the reveal matters.

## Core idea

The backwards white sign creates the unanswered question. The opening copy
names the emotional/product payoff without revealing the surname:

**THEIR NEW SURNAME**  
**DESERVES A STREET SIGN**

The finished sign answers it. Final CTA:

**PERSONALISE THEIRS →**

Suggested caption:

`Two names. One date. Their own street sign. Personalise yours.`

Do not claim that a customer ordered this Reel unless the displayed wording
and claim are genuinely linked and safe to use.

## Eight-second timeline

| Time | Picture | Edit text | Sound purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.00-0.35 s | Synthetic Max already holding the real-looking plain white back; no idle dead air | Hook appears in the top safe area | Quiet workshop bed and a small attention tick |
| 0.35-4.80 s | One uninterrupted monotonic 180° turn; hands and sign remain continuously visible | Hook clears before the front becomes readable | One restrained physical turn/whoosh that follows the motion |
| 4.80-5.30 s | Printed front settles large and level | No text over the product | Soft physical settle/click |
| 5.30-8.00 s | Exact sign holds readable for at least 2.7 seconds | CTA appears outside the sign surface from 5.15 s | Short reveal lift, then room tone |

## Typography and placement

- Match Daisy Maison's existing Instagram-native treatment: bold white
  sans-serif, restrained black stroke/shadow, centred in the upper safe area.
- Keep copy short enough to read once at normal phone speed.
- Never cover Max's eyes, hands, mounting holes, border or lettering.
- The CTA may sit below the sign only when clear space exists; otherwise keep
  it above the product.
- No graphic, caption box or effect may recolour or obscure the sign surface.

## Acceptance logic

- The hook is visible immediately and makes the personalisation relevant.
- The visual reveal answers the hook without narration from Max.
- The exact sign is large/readable for the final 2.7 seconds.
- The CTA asks for one action: personalise the sign.
- Hook, CTA, sound and encoding are added only after the untouched native
  product video passes QC.
