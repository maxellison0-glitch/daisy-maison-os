The idea, in one line

A tuxedo. A sign held back-to-camera. One slow turn — and Mr & Mrs Bond is introduced. It reads as a reveal, not an advert. The joke is the surname; the craft is the real product surviving the turn intact.

One producer’s flag: keep it Bond‑flavoured, not Bond‑branded

“James Bond”, 007, the gun‑barrel and the Monty Norman/John Barry theme are protected by EON/Danjaq/MGM — off‑limits for a commercial post. This concept stays safe: a generic tux + spy‑style reveal, a cleared/original sting, and copy that winks on the couple’s own surname. The bolder “The name? Bond.” line is offered as an optional caption only — your call, and I’d keep it off the burned‑in graphic.
