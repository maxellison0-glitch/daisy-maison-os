- the source sign silhouette, broad black printed front band, white inset,
  mounting holes, material, hand contact, perspective and lighting remain
  recognisably the same;
- the complete Jannaway wording, heart and small line are correct and legible;
- the new printing belongs to the photographed surface, with no pasted panel,
  different white, seam or floating overlay; and
- the model does not reveal, identify or invent the cropped holder.

image becomes the product authority for the synthetic-Max hero-image stage,
which still requires human approval before any paid video generation.
