four untouched Higgsfield/Nano Banana 2 outputs look like photographed Daisy

## What the experiment proves

- Making the real photographed sign the single manufactured-object authority
  is substantially more reliable than asking the model to rebuild geometry
  from a flat SVG plus prose and multiple construction references.
- The model can change the personalised wording while retaining convincing
  printed-surface lighting, texture, black-border weight and physical hand
  contact.
- Four identical prompt runs gave four coherent products, so the correction is
  repeatable at the broad product-realism level.

## What it does not prove

- The exact heart raster survives unchanged.
- One of these images is human-approved.
- Synthetic Max can hold this exact product without changing it.
- A native 180-degree continuous video preserves the face, edge and white
  reverse frame by frame.
