## Method

1. the approved start image;
2. the approved end image;
3. the approved product photograph;
4. real front-to-edge, back-to-edge and white-back product crops;
5. a face-excluded crop of the real Instagram turn for hand choreography,
   physical perspective and thickness only.

The video is generated natively. No product-face overlay, corner pin, mask,
tracked replacement or textured plate is permitted after generation.

## Acceptance

The raw output is rejected before finishing if any frame shows changed
lettering, a black reverse, an invented thick edge, a bent sign, failed hands,
identity drift, non-monotonic rotation, a hidden cut or a product-surface
replacement look.

Finishing may add only whole-shot colour treatment, hook/CTA outside the
product, sound, encoding and delivery scaling.
