## Why the earlier v02 result was rejected

1. The Seedance source still already contained a locally overlaid exact face.
   An artificial composite was therefore used as the video reference.
2. The finished video overlays/replaces the sign face again. The replacement
   panel is visibly whiter than the generated border and does not share its
   reflections, falloff, blur, edge or contact shadows. It looks pasted on.
3. No real edge/thickness views were supplied. Seedance invented a plaque that
   is much too thick during the turn.
4. No real reverse view was supplied. Seedance invented a black back; Daisy
   Maison signs have a white back.
5. Automated lettering and continuity checks passed but failed to assess the
   physical object as humans see it.

Any one of these faults invalidates the complete take. They cannot be repaired
with another overlay.

## Validated workflow

Read `../../WEDDING_SIGN_VIDEO_RULES.md`.

Before another paid video:

1. Build the chosen sign wording as the exact SVG.
2. Lock one real Daisy Maison sign photograph as the manufactured product.
   Give Higgsfield the exact SVG/LightBurn face only as replacement printing
   and generate inexpensive identical-prompt image takes.
3. Do not composite the SVG over a candidate. A human must approve one image in
   which the exact personalised face already reads as the real physical sign.
4. Supply a real-product reference pack containing printed front,
   back-to-edge, front-to-edge and white back. DM-C017 now has this pack from
   one real continuous Daisy Maison turn.
5. Use the approved product image to generate and approve a synthetic-Max hero
   image without changing the product.
6. Generate one native, continuous video from the approved hero image and
   reference pack.
7. Inspect the untouched native video at normal phone speed and frame by frame.
8. Reject/regenerate if lettering, border colour, thickness, white back,
   material, hands or motion changes. Never patch the product surface.

Hook, CTA, captions, timing and sound may be added only away from the sign.

The first passing run used:

- approved product Take 03;
- approved Hero Take 01;
- approved white-back Start Take 01;
- a face/caption-excluded real motion crop;
- one 72-credit Seedance 2.0 job;
- all-frame and every-readable-frame QC;
- top hook, bottom CTA and original sound outside the sign.
