# Creative brief

## Working title

From This Day Forward

## Format

- Primary: 9:16 vertical short video
- Platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels
- First proof: silent technical animatic
- Intended finished duration: approximately 8-10 seconds

## Audience

UK wedding-gift shoppers looking for something personal, sentimental and
already presented as a gift.

## Single idea

Two illustrated hearts become the two people preserved in pebbles.

## Emotional mechanism

The match cut turns the box artwork into the physical relationship represented
inside it. It is metaphorical without hiding what the customer receives.

## Desired feel

- romantic but not sugary;
- tactile and quietly magical;
- realistic, calm and premium;
- frictionless rather than visually busy;
- unmistakably Daisy Maison.

## Story spine

| Time | Picture | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0-2.0s | Tight crop on the paired-heart lid illustration; extremely slow push | Immediate symbolic hook |
| 2.0s | Hard positional match cut | The money moment |
| 2.0-5.0s | Tight crop on the two real pebble people beneath the heart tree | Let the transformation register |
| 5.0-9.0s | Controlled pull-back to the real acrylic heart in its open box | Reveal the gift honestly |
| 9.0-10.0s | Clean product hold | Space for a later CTA or sonic signature |

Timings are hypotheses. The emotional clarity of the match overrides exact
seconds.

## Guardrails

- Product truth outranks spectacle.
- The printed art, dates, script, pebble count and pebble positions stay exact.
- No price, delivery, stock or review claim in the first proof.
- No additional explanatory text until the visual story works muted.
- The master photograph stays untouched; edits are nondestructive derivatives.

## Back burner

`Family Is a Gift` remains a strong separate advert: baby bird on a family box
lid to its corresponding pebble child. It resumes only after a matching family
heart and box are photographed together as one truthful master scene.
