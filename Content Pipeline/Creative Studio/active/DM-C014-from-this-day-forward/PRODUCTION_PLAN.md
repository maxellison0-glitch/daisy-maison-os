# Production plan

## Phase 1 - deterministic animatic

Use the single packaged JPEG. No image generation and no Higgsfield.

Create three nondestructive virtual camera moves:

### Shot A - symbol

- Frame the two small tied hearts printed near the base of the lid artwork.
- Preserve enough botanical linework and `From this day forward` context to
  make the surface read as a gift-box lid.
- Push by approximately 2-3 percent only.
- Do not sharpen or upscale with a model that may redraw lettering.

### Shot B - people

- Frame the two pebble people beneath the heart-shaped tree.
- Align their combined centre, scale and vertical position to the paired hearts
  in Shot A.
- Use a hard cut on the closest shape match. No morph or dissolve.
- Hold long enough for the viewer to understand the metaphor.

### Shot C - gift

- Pull back from the pebble people to the acrylic heart resting inside its real
  open white box.
- Favour product legibility over showing every prop in the square photograph.
- If the intact square composition is used, place it on a simple sampled
  warm-neutral canvas. Do not generatively extend the photograph.

## Phase 1 output

- 9:16 draft MP4
- silent
- phone-review copy permitted at 720x1280
- contact sheet showing the cut alignment
- derivatives in `working/`; review exports in `exports/`

## Phase 2 - only after Max approves the cut

- source the full-resolution original;
- decide whether a separately filmed real fingertip insert adds anything;
- design tactile foley: soft surface contact followed by a restrained stone
  tick at the hard cut;
- add music, copy and CTA only if they improve rather than explain the edit;
- derive organic and paid-safe versions from the same approved picture lock.

## Pixel-level invariants

Never alter:

- the two pebble shapes, count or placement;
- the acrylic heart boundary or ribbon;
- the printed tree, heart, date or handwritten personalisation;
- the lid illustration or `From this day forward` wording;
- box geometry;
- the linen and pale-wood colour relationship.

Any scaling operation must be visually checked for text corruption before a
version is shown as final.
