# Daisy Maison — Content Strategy: The Reason to Post

*The thinking layer above the pipeline. Read this before briefing any reel.*
*Started 23 July 2026. This is the "why", not the "how" (that's `README.md`).*

---

## 1. Why we post (the reason — tied to money, not likes)

Organic sign-reveal reels are Daisy Maison's **cheapest, highest-leverage growth engine**, for three reasons that all show up in the real numbers:

1. **Social is already the #1 traffic source** (36–43% of sessions on strong days) — ahead of paid. The content isn't a side project; it's the top of the funnel.
2. **It carries dark social.** "Direct" traffic runs neck-and-neck with Social (~27% each) — a large chunk is WhatsApp/DM shares of signs people found funny or gift-worthy. A shareable sign is a link that spreads with zero ad spend.
3. **It holds the off-season floor.** The £1M year is decided by the off-peak weeks (£12k → £15k/week is the gap). Paid spend tapers between seasons; organic reach doesn't. Reels are how evergreen sign demand stays warm from June to October.

**So every post has a JOB tied to a number.** No filler. If a post doesn't drive reach, saves, or clicks, it doesn't get made.

---

## 2. The Match Law (the mechanic everything hangs on)

> A sign reel works when the **on-screen hook and the sign are the same idea, revealed in two beats.**
> The hook **opens a loop** (a question, a setup, a feeling). The turn **closes it** (the answer, the punchline, the payoff).
> **The dopamine is the close.**

This is the thing that has to "click." The format (you turning a sign to camera) is just the delivery. What makes one turn get 2k shares and another get ignored is whether the hook and the sign **pay each other off**.

**Failure mode:** a generic hook slapped on a mismatched sign. No loop → no reason to watch → no reveal payoff → scroll.

**The rule of thumb:** write the SIGN and the HOOK as a matched pair. One sets up, the other lands. Test it by covering the sign — does the hook make you *need* to see it? If not, they don't lock yet.

Examples of a locked pair:
- Hook *"POV: your mate said he'd bring the drinks"* → sign **"YOU'RE NOT WELCOME (unless you've brought snacks)"**. (setup → punchline)
- Hook *"Their new surname deserves a street sign"* → sign **"MR & MRS BOND"**. (feeling → payoff)
- Hook *"What do you get the dad who has everything?"* → sign **"[SURNAME] STADIUM"**. (question → answer)

## 3. The three engines (each family → one engine → one job)

Every sign maps to one of three engines. The engine decides the hook, the metric, and the posting job.

| Engine | Sign families | Hook shape | Job / metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LAUGH** | Home Bar, Family Kitchen, "rules"/funny, man-cave, pub | Relatable setup / "POV: …" / callout | **Reach + shares + tags** (dark social). Metric: sends, shares, comments |
| **FEEL** | Mr & Mrs (wedding), Family, New Home, memorial, baby | Emotional setup ("their new…") | **Saves + gifting intent**. Metric: saves, profile visits |
| **SOLVE** | Father's Day (Stadium, Bar & Grill, number plate), Teacher, Wedding, New Home, Christmas | "What do you get the person who…" / deadline | **Convert the searcher**. Metric: link clicks, saves |

**Mix (per the pipeline's 70/20/10):** lean LAUGH + FEEL for reach and saves; use SOLVE when there's a live occasion or deadline to convert. Humour is the top-of-funnel spread; feel/solve is where the gifting money is.

---

## 4. The POV library (the "actual POVs" — reusable openers)

The turn is the mechanic; the POV is the frame around it. Rotate these so the feed isn't one note:

1. **Straight-turn reveal** — hold the back, hook on screen, turn → payoff. The default.
2. **"POV: you [relatable situation]"** → turn to the sign that speaks to it. (LAUGH)
3. **Packing-bench / "just made this for a customer"** → turn/reveal. Real-order flavour, builds trust. (FEEL/SOLVE)
4. **"Tag someone who…"** → the sign is the callout. Pure share engine. (LAUGH)
5. **"Guess who this is for"** → turn reveal. Curiosity loop. (FEEL)
6. **Two-sign choice** — hold two, *"which one?"* → comments engine. (any)
7. **Reply-to-comment / "you asked, we made it"** → make the sign a viewer requested. Community + proof. (any)

Each POV is just a way to open the loop the sign will close. Pick the POV that best sets up *that* sign.

## 5. Production routing (use the right tool — we now have two)

We can now make a reel **two** ways. Using the wrong one wastes time or credits.

- **REAL film** — you physically turn a real stock sign you already have. Fastest, most authentic, zero credits. **Default for evergreen humour + family signs** (Home Bar, Family Kitchen, "rules"). This is the original winning format; keep doing it — nothing looks more native than the real thing.
- **SYNTHETIC pipeline** (the DM-C018 method) — for signs you do **not** physically have: a specific personalised name, a themed one-off (Bond), an occasion sign you can't stock. The superpower is **infinite personalised reveals without producing the sign**. Reserve it for variety, personalisation-at-scale, and themed concepts.

**Rule: if you can film it in two minutes, film it.** Synthetic is for what you can't film.

**Caveat (production dev backlog):** the synthetic method is currently *validated only for the white wedding-sign family*. Extending it to other families (black home-bar, coloured family signs, different layouts) each needs its own validated real-sign authority + exact artwork build + one validation run before it can be trusted on video. Until then, non-wedding synthetic is a **build task**, and **real filming covers those families today**. Priority order to build synthetic support, by demand: Family/Home → Home Bar → Football/Number-plate.

---

## 6. What "a true social media presence" looks like (the standard)

- Every reel is a **matched pair** (Section 2) with a named **job** (Section 3) before it's filmed.
- A steady rotation across the three engines and the POV library, so the feed has range but one recognisable voice.
- Winners are **remade** with a new sign/occasion/hook — not reposted.
- The `PUBLISH_LOG` records hook/hold, shares/saves, and the exact audience language from comments; that language becomes the next batch of hooks.
- Humour buys the reach; feel/solve banks the gifting demand that holds the off-season floor.
