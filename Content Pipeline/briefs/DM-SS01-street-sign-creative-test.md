## Product-truth guardrails

- Use an exact verified Daisy source image as the reference for every generation.
- Preserve the flat UV-printed acrylic construction and printed border.
- Do not invent a physical raised frame, new materials, wording, reviews or packaging.
- Keep the surname and date readable and keep all foreground objects away from the sign.
- Reject any output with changed lettering, incorrect proportions or malformed text before Canva work begins.

## Testing discipline

- Compare the four buying motivations before testing small colour changes.
- Hold audience, objective, offer, landing page and placement constant inside the test.
- Judge each pair on thumb-stop rate, three-second hold, outbound CTR and cost per landing-page view/purchase.
- Once a concept wins, iterate its opening frame, hook, CTA and colour treatment separately.
