# DM-C013 — Family Is a Gift

## Brief

- **State:** Editing — technical animatic
- **Job:** Stop the scroll and solve a family-gift problem
- **Primary audience and occasion:** UK shoppers choosing a sentimental personalised family gift
- **Single promise or question:** A printed symbol of family becomes their actual pebble family.
- **Why this is worth watching:** The baby-bird-to-pebble-person match cut makes the existing gift box and personalised heart feel like one intentional gift.
- **Primary format:** Short vertical video
- **Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels
- **Target publish time:** Not scheduled; concept proof first

## Locked transition test

The first deliverable is only a four-second technical animatic:

1. exact `Family is a gift that lasts forever` box artwork;
2. temporary fingertip guide touches the smaller baby bird;
3. hard contact cut to the same fingertip position on a small pebble person;
4. pull back to reveal the family heart inside an open white box.

The viewer mentally supplies the opening action. No AI model is asked to
generate a lid opening, redraw the products, or preserve moving typography.

## Storyboard

| Time | Visual | Audio | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.5s | Exact closed box holds in a warm vertical composition | Quiet room tone | Establish product immediately |
| 0.5–1.6s | Temporary fingertip guide approaches and touches the smaller baby bird | Soft skin-on-texture brush | Create tactile anticipation |
| 1.6s | Hard cut on contact to the small pebble person at the identical screen coordinate | Paper tap paired with stone tick | Metaphorical transformation |
| 1.6–4.2s | Camera pulls back from pebble macro to heart inside open box | Restrained reveal breath | Reveal the complete gift |
| 4.2–4.5s | Open-box composition holds | Audio resolves | Let the idea register |
