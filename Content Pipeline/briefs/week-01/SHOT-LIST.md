# Week 1 — single capture session

Goal: collect the real material needed to finish DM-C006 and unlock DM-C008 to
DM-C011. Do not film customer names, order screens, addresses, or messages.

## Capture rules for organic and paid reuse

- Clean the phone lens; use the rear camera.
- Film vertical at the highest practical quality, ideally 4K/30 if available.
- Keep the product centred with generous space around it so the same take can
  crop to 9:16, 4:5, and 1:1.
- Hold each shot steady for two seconds before and after the action.
- Record real typing, workshop, paper, ribbon, and product-handling sound.
- Avoid platform music during filming; music and voice remain separate edit layers.
- Use fictional or permissioned personalisation only.
- Check every frame for labels, screens, paperwork, or parcels containing data.

Do not rename or overwrite the selected take after it enters an approved edit.
