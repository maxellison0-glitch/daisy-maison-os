# DM-C008 — Made and Checked

## Brief

- **State:** Briefed
- **Job:** Build trust
- **Primary audience:** Buyers suspicious of mockups or mass-produced gifts
- **Single promise:** Show the real object and the human quality check.
- **Primary format:** 15–18-second vertical video
- **Platforms:** TikTok first; Instagram and Facebook adaptations

## Hook options

1. `This isn't a mockup.`
2. `Most people only see the finished gift.`
3. `The check every personalised sign gets.`

## Storyboard

| Beat | Visual | Sound-off copy | Audio | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hands lift finished real sign | `This isn't a mockup.` | Real workshop sound | Hook/product |
| 2 | Printing or making detail | `Made for their names.` | Machine sound | Proof |
