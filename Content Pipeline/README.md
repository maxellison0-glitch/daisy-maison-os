# Daisy Maison Content Pipeline

This is the single entry point for organic Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok
content. Its job is simple: turn Daisy's real products, personalisation, making,
packing, and customer occasions into four strong posts each week, with a fifth
only when the idea is worth making.

## Start here every time

1. Open `PRODUCTION_QUEUE.md` and continue the first item with a clear next
   action.
2. If the queue is empty, choose four ideas from `IDEAS.md` using the weekly
   mix below.
3. Create one brief per chosen idea from `templates/POST_TEMPLATE.md`.
4. Gather real footage or photographs before generating substitutes.
5. Produce one master concept, then adapt the hook, edit, caption, and CTA for
   each platform.
6. Show Max the finished preview. Nothing is posted without his explicit
   approval.
7. Upload the approved local files directly to OneUp, verify every intended
   queue entry once, and log the result in `PUBLISH_LOG.md`.
8. Record the 48-hour result, then the 7-day result. Feed the lesson into the
   next batch.

## Who's writing and fronting this

Two characters front the account: Max (real founder) and Freya (proposed
social/creative lead). Their voice, personality and content roles live in
`Personas/README.md` — read it before briefing any post that needs a caption
voice or an on-camera presence.

## Pipeline states

`Saved -> Selected -> Briefed -> Assets ready -> Editing -> Ready for approval -> Approved -> Scheduled -> Published -> Learned`

Every active row must name the next physical action. Never leave an item as
"working on it."

## Weekly content mix

Use four core posts, plus one optional trend or seasonal post:

| Slot | Job | Strong Daisy format |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stop the scroll | Personalisation transformation: blank to finished |
| 2 | Invite a choice | Two or three designs, sizes, messages, or gift options |
| 3 | Build trust | Making, quality check, packing, or order journey |
| 4 | Solve a gift problem | Occasion-led recommendation or buying mistake |
| 5 optional | Catch attention | A native trend, reaction, timely deadline, or customer story |

This is a starting structure, not a quota. Do not publish filler to complete a
calendar.

A practical default is Monday transformation, Tuesday choice, Thursday
behind-the-scenes, and Saturday gift problem. Use Wednesday or Sunday only for
the optional timely post. Change the days once the account's own data shows a
better pattern.

## Creative rules

- Show the gift, occasion, or transformation in the first frame. Do not begin
  with a logo animation.
- Give each post one story, question, or decision.
- Prefer real hands, workshop footage, screen personalisation, product detail,
  packing, and reveals. Daisy has better raw material than generic stock or
  animated catalogue panels.
- Write hooks about the buyer's problem: what to buy, which version to choose,
  whether it will arrive in time, and how personal it will feel.
- Make the opening understandable with sound off. Captions must be burned in
  and checked on a phone-sized preview.
- For 1080 x 1920 video, keep primary reading copy roughly inside y=360-1430.
  Use the top only for light branding and the bottom only for non-essential
  content so TikTok and Reels UI cannot bury the message.
- Change the visual or audio information regularly when it helps the story.
  A reveal, cut, close-up, pointer, or restrained sound cue is enough.
- Use customer names, messages, addresses, order screens, or artwork only after
  replacing or anonymising all identifying information.
- Do not invent reviews, delivery promises, product details, discounts, stock,
  prices, or manufacturing steps. Verify anything changeable before posting.
- Do not hard-sell every post. Aim for roughly 70% useful/engaging, 20%
  proof/process, and 10% direct offer or deadline.

## Platform adaptation

Do not blindly publish the identical wrapper everywhere. Reuse the idea and
assets, then adapt them.

### TikTok

- Native, immediate, and human; normally 12-25 seconds.
- Lead with a reveal, process, opinion, or choice.
- Use concise on-screen copy and ask a replyable question.
- Slightly imperfect real footage is often stronger than a polished advert.

### Instagram

- Reels for process, reveals, and emotion; carousels for choices, guides, and
  saveable occasion ideas.
- Make the cover clear at grid size and keep the product visually beautiful.
- Optimise for saves, shares, and profile visits, not only likes.

### Facebook

- Give slightly more context and make the gifting situation relatable.
- Clear occasion, recipient, deadline, or decision-led posts are strongest.
- Invite comments and shares naturally. Use links only when the post genuinely
  helps a shopper take the next step.

## Production and approval gates

Before approval:

- Confirm the product and personalisation are accurate.
- Watch once with sound and once muted.
- Check the first two seconds, spelling, pacing, centre-safe text, cover, and
  final CTA on a phone-sized preview.
- Freeze the approved export. Do not silently overwrite the version that was
  approved or posted.

After approval:

- Before the first run, connect and visibly name the correct Daisy Facebook
  Page, Instagram business account, and TikTok account in OneUp. Never assume
  that another project's connected profiles are Daisy's.
- Upload finished local media directly inside OneUp.
- Select only the intended Daisy accounts.
- Verify one scheduled or published entry per intended platform.
- If OneUp returns an error, check Scheduled, Published, and Failed before any
  retry. Never blind-retry; that previously caused a duplicate TikTok post.
- Report failures immediately instead of inventing a workaround.

## What success means

Judge ideas by the signal they are meant to create:

- TikTok/Reels: first-second hold, average watch time, completion, rewatches,
  shares, saves, comments, and profile visits.
- Instagram carousels: reach, second-slide continuation, saves, shares,
  comments, and profile visits.
- Facebook: three-second views, watch time, shares, comments, and useful link
  clicks when a link is present.

At 48 hours, label the result `Weak`, `Promising`, or `Winner` relative to the
account's recent baseline. At 7 days, record the lasting result and one lesson.
Remake winners with a new product, occasion, or hook. Do not merely repost the
same creative.

## Default weekly batch

1. Generate 8-10 concepts from real current products, occasions, comments,
   FAQs, and production activity.
2. Select four with different jobs from the weekly mix.
3. Brief all four before editing any of them.
4. Capture all required real footage in one shot list.
5. Build, preview, and approve each post.
6. Schedule across the correct Daisy accounts and verify every entry.
7. Review performance before selecting the next batch.

The pipeline automates repeatable work, not judgment. Max approves the final
creative and every external post.
