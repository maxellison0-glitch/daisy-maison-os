# Freya's Reference Accounts — "the accounts we copy"

*Copy = **aesthetic and format only** (framing, pacing, hooks, on-screen text
treatment, grade, reveal mechanics). Never copy captions, claims, or another
brand's personalised customer content.*

Freya reads this list at the start of every session, checks what each posted
recently, and pulls 3–5 "borrow this" moves — each mapped back to our own
turn-around videos and street signs. **She verifies/updates the handles and
adds/removes accounts as she learns** (that's part of adapting). Handles
marked `?` are to be confirmed on first visit.

## How Freya uses each (the translation rule)
For every borrowed move she writes: **"[Account] did [specific move] → we do it
as [specific sign reel: POV + hook + which sign]."** If a trick can't become
"Max or Freya turning a sign," it's not for us that day.

## Rules
- Aesthetic/format only. No lifting captions, no copying a rival's customer names.
- Prefer what's *currently* getting engagement over evergreen-but-stale looks.
- Log the source next to any idea it inspired (traceability in the brief).
- Keep this list to ~8–12 live accounts; prune ruthlessly as taste sharpens.
