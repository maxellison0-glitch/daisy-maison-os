# Daisy Maison Personas

The two characters who front Daisy Maison's social content: **Max**, the
real founder, and **Freya**, a proposed social/creative-lead character to be
built as a consistent Higgsfield identity.

This folder is the planning layer — who they are, how they sound, how she
gets built. Producing an actual piece of content still runs through the
existing `Content Pipeline/Creative Studio` process once it's briefed.

## Start here

1. `MAX.md` — the real founder's on-camera voice and content role. Already
   true, not proposed.
2. `FREYA.md` — the proposed second character: full identity, voice,
   positioning, appearance spec. **Not yet approved** — read this and
   confirm or redirect the name/direction before anything is built.
3. `VOICE_AND_CAPTION_GUIDE.md` — the shared caption and tone playbook both
   characters write from. Usable immediately, no approval needed — it's a
   writing standard, not a creative commitment.
4. `HIGGSFIELD_CHARACTER_BUILD.md` — the accurate, staged workflow to
   actually build Freya as a consistent Higgsfield Soul character, once her
   direction is approved. Phased and credit-conscious; nothing in it should
   run unattended.

## Why this exists

Creative Studio already proves Daisy Maison can make excellent AI-assisted
product video — see DM-C017's validated wedding-sign turn. What was missing
was a *person*: someone whose voice writes the captions and decides what
gets posted, the way a real social media manager would. Max fronts the
craft/trust side of the account; Freya (proposed) fronts the taste/curation
side. Neither replaces the other — see each file for their distinct content
roles.

## Approval state

- **Max** — live, no approval needed. This documents an existing real
  person's on-camera voice.
- **Freya** — proposed. Name, look and positioning are all in `FREYA.md`,
  ready to approve or redirect. No Higgsfield credits should be spent
  training her identity until that happens.
- **Voice/caption guide** — usable immediately by anyone writing for the
  account.

Scheduling and publishing-connector integration is intentionally out of
scope here — noted as a future step, not handled in this folder.
