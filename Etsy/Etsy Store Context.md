## Etsy SEO vs Website SEO — The Relationship

Daisy Maison runs **two parallel SEO channels**:

- **Etsy SEO** — driven by Etsy search algorithm (recency, tags, conversion rate, reviews)
- **Google SEO** — driven by the Daisy Maison website (daisymaison.co.uk)

**Key principle:** Etsy listings and website product pages should target the same buyer intent but with platform-appropriate optimisation. Etsy titles use keyword stacking. Website pages use natural language and depth.
