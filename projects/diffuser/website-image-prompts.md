# Personalised Mr & Mrs Reed Diffuser — website image prompts

Turn the raw phone photos into clean, website-ready product images by
**restaging the surroundings only**. Same discipline as the signs: the product
and its label are sacred. Use as **image edits** (reference = the raw photo) in
Higgsfield Nano Banana 2 / Nano Banana Pro (Pro handles fine text best).

## The product (lock this exactly — never redraw it)
- Square, heavy **clear-glass** diffuser bottle; short neck.
- **Oxblood / dark-burgundy metallic collar** (the cap ring).
- ~8 slim matte **black reed sticks**, fanned.
- **Personalised black label**, cream/ivory keyline border, white text:
  top small boxed line **"HOME BY DAISY MAISON"**, centre **"MR & MRS"** above
  the surname **"Smith"** in white script, base line **"TOGETHER WE MAKE SCENTS"**.
- Gift set: **cream/ivory rigid lidded box**, **taupe grosgrain ribbon + bow**,
  blank **kraft swing tag**, **kraft crinkle-paper** filler.

## PRODUCT LOCK — paste at the top of every prompt
```
EDIT THIS PHOTOGRAPH. Keep the product itself 100% unchanged and pixel-faithful:
do not redraw, restyle, recolour, reshape, resize, rotate or move the diffuser
bottle, its oxblood metal collar, or the black reeds. The printed label must stay
EXACTLY as photographed — every letter, the exact wording "HOME BY DAISY MAISON",
"MR & MRS", the script surname "Smith", and "TOGETHER WE MAKE SCENTS", the cream
keyline border and white type — all perfectly legible and identical. Do not
regenerate, re-typeset, translate, blur or paraphrase any text. Change ONLY the
background, surface, lighting and styling around the product. Photorealistic,
premium product photography — no illustration, no added text, logos or watermarks.
```

## 1 — Hero on clean seamless (primary listing image)
```
[PRODUCT LOCK]
Place the diffuser on a clean seamless warm-white/putty studio background, shot
straight-on at a natural product-photography angle. Soft, even diffused studio
light from the front-left, a gentle realistic soft shadow and a faint reflection

Warm neutral palette, crisp focus on the label, subtle depth. Vertical 4:5.

## Notes (read before generating)
- **Label fidelity is the risk.** Fine text is where AI editing fails. Generate
  4 cheap takes per shot, then **zoom the label and verify every word** before
  using any image — reject any take that alters the type, exactly like a sign QC.
- Nano Banana **Pro** renders text best; prefer it for shots 1, 4, 5 where the
  label is prominent. ~2 credits per 2k take.
- For the true macro **label close-up**, prefer a real re-crop of the sharp photo
  over AI — safest text of all.
- "Smith" is the sample personalisation. Swap in a different sample name only by
  re-shooting/re-labelling the real bottle — never by asking AI to change the text.
- These restage the *surroundings*; they are honest product images (the real
  product, cleaner room), consistent with the brand's locked
  `projects/social-prompts/PROMPT.md` rule.
