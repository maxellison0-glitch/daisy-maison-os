Before you sell

Once you cut a soap loaf or rebottle the oil, you're making a new pack. Every slice and bottle you sell then needs the full cosmetic label. That covers:

  • the name and what it is
  • net weight or volume
  • the full ingredients list
  • warnings
  • a batch code and best-before date
  • the name and UK address of the Responsible Person

The bath bomb boxes already carry all of this, so they're fine as they are. Just don't cover or remove AW's label.

Email AW these two questions before you print anything:

  1. "Can you supply a safety report (CPSR) that covers me slicing your soap loaves, and rebottling MOB-04 into 100 ml glass, under my brand? Will you stay the Responsible Person, or will I be?"
  2. "Please send the full ingredients and warnings for MOB-04, SLHCS-57, SLHCS-06 and SLHCS-13. Part of the oil label is cut off in my photo."

If you become the Responsible Person, each product also has to be notified on GOV.UK ("Submit cosmetic product notifications") before it goes on sale.

  • No treatment claims. "Muscle Ease" is AW's name for the blend. Describe it as a fresh massage blend for after exercise, and never say it "relieves pain" or "reduces inflammation".
  • Nothing that looks edible. Keep cocktail glasses, real drinks and food props out of every image. Cosmetics that look like food are a safety issue, and alcohol imagery can get Meta ads restricted.
  • Emerald Truffle: its patchouli comes from the perfume ("derived from parfum" on the label), so don't write "with patchouli oil". Say "emerald truffle scent".

Personalised Massage Oil, Muscle Ease Blend, 100 ml

£14.95
What it is
100 ml of MOB-04 in your black glass dropper bottle, with a personalised front label. It's made the same way as your reed diffusers (also £14.95), so the two sit together as one range.
Blend
Grapeseed oil with lavender, mint (Mentha arvensis) and eucalyptus essential oils. Confirm the full list with AW.
Yield
1 L fills 9 to 10 bottles once you allow for spills. Order more oil before you run ads.
Bottle
Black glass, 100 ml (check the base or your invoice), glass pipette, black cap. The neck is probably 18 mm (DIN 18). A pipette is slow for massage oil. An 18 mm black disc-top or lotion pump is easier to use and costs pennies, but it's optional.
Seal for posting
PVC shrink band over the cap (18 mm neck; shrink it with a hairdryer), then bubble wrap. Dropper tops can weep in transit.
Front label
About 45 × 60 mm, personalised: Daisy Maison logo, name line, message line, then "Massage Oil · Muscle Ease Blend · 100 ml". Measure the straight side of your bottle first; a 100 ml bottle is usually about 45 mm across.
Back label
About 45 × 60 mm, the legal panel: ingredients, warnings, batch code, best before, Responsible Person with UK address, and 100 ml. Use the warnings on AW's label: harmful if swallowed, avoid contact with eyes, seek medical advice if swallowed. Add "For external use only", "Patch test before use" and "Keep out of reach of children", plus anything else AW's safety report lists.
Label stock
Oil-proof white or kraft polypropylene (vinyl). Oil turns paper labels see-through.
Personalisation
Line 1: a name, up to 14 characters (e.g. "Dad"). Line 2: a message, up to 28 characters (e.g. "For after the long run").
Who it's for
Father's Day, birthdays, runners, gym-goers, gardeners, partners. Avoid pregnancy and new-mum angles (mint and eucalyptus) unless AW's safety report says otherwise.

Soap Slices: Emerald Truffle, Slice of Sunshine, Rose & Rose Petals

£4.95 each · trio £12.95
Yield
3 loaves × 13 = 39 slices of about 100 g.
Cutting
Cut along AW's 100 g scoring with a soap or cheese wire (or a long, sharp knife) and weigh every slice. Label them "100 g" only if each one weighs at least that. Keep light slices as samples or freebies.
Seal
These are glycerin soaps (the ingredients start with water and glycerin), so they sweat in open air. Shrink-wrap each slice with a heat gun, or seal it in a clear cello bag, before adding the band.
Belly band
Kraft, 40 mm wide. The length is the distance round the slice plus 15 mm overlap (measure it; probably 180–230 mm). Front: Daisy Maison, the scent name, "Cut by hand · 100 g". Back: ingredients, batch, best before, Responsible Person.
Trio box
One of each scent in a kraft box sized to three slices stacked side by side. Measure the slices before ordering.
True to the label
Slice of Sunshine: orange scent, topped with dried orange slices. Rose & Rose Petals: made with real rose petals. Emerald Truffle: emerald truffle scent, with a soft shimmer from mica (only claim the shimmer if you can see it).

Photos ChatGPT needs from you

ChatGPT has to work from the real, finished product. Without photos it invents one you don't sell, which means refunds and rejected ads. For each finished item (bottle labelled, slice banded, box with its tag), shoot in daylight on a plain white or cream surface, without flash:

  • front, straight on
  • 45° angle
  • top-down
  • the label, close enough to read
  • one in your hand, for scale

Also attach one of the gift wrap kit hero images (e.g. the Midnight one) as the style reference, and a photo of a reed diffuser label so the new labels match it.
