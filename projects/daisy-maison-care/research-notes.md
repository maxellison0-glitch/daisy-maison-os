# Daisy Maison Care — Launch research

Research checked: 17 August 2026

## Easiest operating model

Use Nutribl for a ten-unit branded pilot, then fulfil through Daisy Maison's existing dispatch workflow. Do not add a separate dropshipping or order-routing integration until demand is proven. This keeps the first test to one supplier, one SKU and one existing Shopify operation.

Nutribl's self-design route is the cheapest label path. The supplied artwork must be emailed to `design@nutribl.com`; Nutribl uploads it to the trade account, and Daisy Maison formally approves the proof online before print.

## Claims guardrail

Only authorised health claims may be used in Great Britain. The label and Shopify preview therefore use the two claims included in Nutribl's `PL-489_V3.docx`:

- Vitamin C contributes to normal collagen formation for the normal function of skin, bones and cartilage.
- Riboflavin, biotin and niacin contribute to the maintenance of normal skin.

Do not use claims about reducing wrinkles, anti-ageing, plumper skin, hydration, treating joint pain, or preventing disease. Competitor language is not a compliance template.
