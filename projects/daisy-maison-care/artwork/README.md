# Daisy Maison Care — PL-489 collagen label

This artwork is built for Nutribl product **PL-489**, the 300 g unflavoured marine collagen powder in the 1000 ml cylinder jar.

## Supplier specification

- Finished label: 200 × 100 mm
- Supplied artwork: 204 × 104 mm (2 mm bleed on every edge)
- Supplier upload format: high-quality RGB JPG
- Resolution: 600 dpi / 4819 × 2457 px
- Minimum text size: 5 pt
- Supplier filename: `PL-489$front.jpg`

The design uses Nutribl's `PL-489_V3.docx` copy, keeps the fish allergen bold, includes all required cautions, and limits marketing statements to the approved claims in the supplier text.

The production JPG was emailed from `orders@daisymaison.co.uk` to `design@nutribl.com` on 17 August 2026. Nutribl must upload it to the trade account before the final online proof can be approved.

## Build

Run `build_label.py` with the bundled Python runtime, then render the PDF at 600 dpi with Poppler. The final JPG is the file to email to `design@nutribl.com`; the PDF is a proof and archival source.

Do not send or approve the artwork for print until the final business-operator details and every line of product copy have been checked against the current Nutribl file.
