# Daisy Maison IG Prompt

Use this exact prompt, every time, for any Daisy Maison product photo.
Paste it into Higgsfield Nano Banana Pro 2 with the photo. No customizing per
photo, no extra steps.

```
Turn this raw product photo into a polished, Instagram-ready shot for Daisy
Maison. Do not change, redraw, retouch, resize, recolor, or move the product
itself in any way — every word of printed or engraved text, every logo, and
every color/shape on the product must stay pixel-accurate and fully legible,
exactly as photographed. Only improve the surroundings: replace any
cluttered, unfinished, or workshop-looking background with a clean, soft,
warm neutral setting (matte cream/putty tones, light natural wood or linen),
add soft natural light with gentle realistic shadows, and blur or remove
background clutter. Shallow depth of field, warm neutral palette, minimal
boutique product-photography look, photorealistic — no illustration/cartoon
style, no added text or logos, no change to the product.
```
