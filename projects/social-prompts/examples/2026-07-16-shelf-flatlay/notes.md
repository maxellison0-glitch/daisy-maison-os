## Pass 1 — Background & Lighting

```
Edit this product photo. Do not touch, move, resize, recolor, or redraw
anything in the foreground/products — the white ceramic cloud-shaped plaques
printed with "The sky's the limit", "Follow your dreams" (x2), and
"Everything is going to be fine", the white ceramic crown, the stacked kraft
gift boxes, and the heart-leaf monstera plant. Only replace the background:
the whitewashed breeze-block wall and bare plywood shelf become a soft matte
cream/putty-toned wall and a light natural wood or linen-draped surface. Add
soft, warm natural window light from the left with gentle realistic shadows.
Remove or softly blur the loose prints and cardboard sheets in the
background. Shallow depth of field, warm neutral pastel palette, minimal
boutique product-photography look. Photorealistic, no illustration/cartoon
rendering, no added logos or text.
```

## Pass 2 — Light Touch-Up

```
Make a light color-grade and crop pass only: warm the tones slightly, tighten
the crop toward the product cluster (plaques, crown, boxes, plant), soften
shadows. Do not alter any product, its shape, its color, or any printed text
in the image — treat everything already in the photo as fixed.
```
