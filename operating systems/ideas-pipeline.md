## Active Ideas

### 1. Microfiber cleaning cloth add-on
- **Signal:** Freestanding range (pebble pictures, number plates) currently attaches nothing. Mounting Strips only work for wall-mounted signs. ~85% of Father's Day baskets attach zero add-ons.
- **Proposed move:** List a microfiber cloth as a checkout upsell, paired primarily with pebble pictures and acrylic plates.
- **Expected impact:** Incremental AOV uplift of £1–2 per qualifying order. Near-100% margin. No cannibalisation of strips.
- **Effort:** Low — cheap to source, easy to pack, no theme change needed. Product listing + Candy Rack config.
- **Status:** 🔵 Proposed
- **Next action:** Source product, confirm unit cost and pack time, create Shopify listing.

### 2. Wooden easel for pebble pictures
- **Signal:** Pebble pictures are a freestanding product with no natural display solution sold alongside them.
- **Proposed move:** Add a small wooden easel as an add-on at checkout for pebble picture orders.
- **Expected impact:** Higher AOV than cloth (£3–5 price point), but lower attach rate due to price sensitivity and packing friction.
- **Effort:** Medium — bulkier to pack, needs shipping-fit check before committing. Product image can be AI-generated.
- **Status:** 🔵 Proposed
- **Next action:** Resolve packing question (does it fit in standard envelope/box?). Only proceed after cloth test validates the freestanding-attach concept.

### 3. Breakdown attach rate by individual product
- **Signal:** Current attach rate data is aggregated. The wall-vs-freestanding theory is directionally confirmed but not granular.
- **Proposed move:** Pull Shopify data to see attach rate per product title — which specific signs have the highest strip attach, and which freestanding products have zero attach.
- **Expected impact:** Pinpoints exactly which products to target with each add-on. Improves targeting of Candy Rack upsell rules.
- **Effort:** Low — analysis only, no storefront change.
- **Status:** 🔵 Proposed
- **Next action:** Run ShopifyQL query grouping orders by product + add-on co-occurrence.
