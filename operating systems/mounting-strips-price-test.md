# Mounting Strips Price Test — Live as of 2026-06-06

## What we did
Raised the Mounting Strips add-on price from **£1.95 → £2.25** (~15% increase) as a controlled profit test.

Two changes made, nothing else touched:
- Variant price: £1.95 → **£2.25**
- Product title: "(£1.95) Mounting Strips" → **(£2.25) Mounting Strips**

## Daily check-in (2–3 weeks)

### Core metrics to watch
| Metric | What to look for |
|---|---|
| **Mounting Strips attach rate %** | Orders with strips ÷ total orders × 100. Watch for a drop >5–10 pp — that's the danger signal. |
| **Mounting Strips units/day** | Absolute volume. Steady or slight drop is fine. |
| **Mounting Strips revenue/day** | Units × £2.25. Should be equal or higher than before. |
| **Average order value** | Should tick up slightly if attach rate holds. |
| **Total orders & revenue** | Make sure the price change isn't affecting conversion more broadly. |
| **ROAS** | Check weekly in Meta/Google — no direct link but worth watching. |

### What success looks like
Revenue per day from Mounting Strips is **equal or higher** than baseline.  
i.e. the 15% price rise is not cancelled out by a >15% drop in attach rate.
