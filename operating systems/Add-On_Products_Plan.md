# Add-On Products & Pricing — Working Plan

*Daisy Maison UK · drafted 6 June 2026*

The thesis: low-cost add-ons are near-pure profit and compound month after month. Mounting Strips alone have done **~6,559 attach orders / ~£15,660** since launching April 2025 (~£1,100/month and rising), on a product that costs pennies. The job now is to (a) price what we have correctly and (b) capture the baskets that currently attach nothing.

---

## What the data told us

- **Attach rate is driven by product type, not the gifting occasion.** It swings from ~10% (summer) to ~44% (Valentine's), but the real pattern is whether the basket is a **wall-mounted street sign** (strips attach) vs a **freestanding item** like pebble pictures or standing acrylic plates (strips irrelevant).
- That's why Valentine's (couples street signs) hit ~42–44% attach, while Mother's Day (pebble pictures, 13.8%) and Father's Day 2025 (number plates / standing signs, ~15%) were *low* despite being big gifting peaks.
- **Conclusion:** the freestanding range is the untapped segment. It currently attaches nothing, so a new add-on there is incremental — not cannibalising strips.

---

## Decisions made

### 1. Test a small price increase on Mounting Strips — £1.95 → £2.25
Don't cut. Strips are a staple with 30%+ attach on high-value sign orders; someone spending £30 on a personalised sign won't blink at 30p more, so demand should be near-inelastic and the extra flows almost entirely to profit. Tiny downside, fully reversible. Run for 2–3 weeks and watch attach rate + revenue per order.

### 2. Microfiber cleaning cloth — new add-on ✅ (loved this one)
- Place **at checkout** as a low-friction upsell.
- Pair it with **pebble pictures** (the freestanding range that attaches nothing today).
- May also work alongside street signs — not essential there, but worth leaving on.
- Cheap to source, easy to pack, near-100% margin. The cleanest first test.

### 3. Wooden easel for pebble pictures — new add-on (promising)
- Natural complement for a freestanding picture.
- **We can generate a product image for it** — no blocker there.
- ⚠️ **Open question: packing.** A wooden easel is bulkier/more awkward than a cloth — needs a check on pack time and shipping fit before committing.

---

## Suggested order of testing

1. **Price increase £1.95 → £2.25** — zero new product work, immediate signal. Start now.
2. **Microfiber cloth at checkout** — cheapest/easiest new product, targets the empty freestanding baskets.
3. **Wooden easel** — once the cloth proves the freestanding-attach idea, and after the packing question is resolved.
