## Weekly Patterns to Know

- **Monday** tends to be slower — weekend browsers converting, but ad spend efficiency is lower.
- **Friday–Sunday** historically stronger for gifting impulse purchases in peak seasons.
- Platform outages (recorded): Shopify down 3 Jun 2026 ~1–3pm; Google Ads offline 2 Jun 2026 all day. Neither day should be used as a benchmark.
