# What's in this folder (plain English)

This is Daisy Maison's "operating systems" folder — the memory and notes your
business runs on. **A few of these files are read by your Morning Digest every day
— those are marked ⚙️. Don't rename or delete the ⚙️ ones or the digest breaks.**
Everything else is just your notes; rename or tidy those freely.

## ⚙️ Used by your Morning Digest automation (leave the names exactly as they are)
- **context.md** — the digest's running memory: today's target, recent results,
  standing knowledge, lessons already taught. It updates this itself each morning.
- **learnings.md** — the lessons the digest has built up over time.
- **mounting-strips-price-test.md** — the live £1.95 → £2.25 price experiment it watches.
- **etsy-latest.md** — where you paste your latest Etsy numbers (from the Etsy tracker
  prompt). The digest reads this and adds an Etsy section. Keep it fresh; don't rename it.

## 📝 Your own notes (safe to rename, move, or tidy)
- **Add-On_Products_Plan.md** — your plan for add-on products (strips, gift wrap, etc.).
- **revenue-levers.md** — ideas/levers for growing revenue.
- **decision-log.md** — a log of business decisions you've made.
- **ideas-pipeline.md** — your ideas backlog.
- **product-design-log.md** — notes from designing products.
- **seasonal-performance.md** — how products do across seasons.
- **weekly-context.md** — weekly context notes.
- **Shopify AI Development Learnings.pdf** — a reference doc.
- **ChatGPT Image … .png** — a stray screenshot. Probably belongs in your photos —
  move it out whenever (it's not used by anything here).

_If you're ever unsure whether something's safe to touch, the ⚙️ files are the only
ones that matter to the automation. The rest is just paperwork._
