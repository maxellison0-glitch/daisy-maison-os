## The Six Levers

Getting more of the right people to the store.

| Sub-lever | Current State | Notes |

| Add-on for freestanding range | Not yet live | Microfiber cloth proposed. Wooden easel proposed. Both in ideas-pipeline. |
| Bundle / gift wrap | Exists | Gift wrap offered. Attach rate not tracked separately. |
| Higher-ticket products | Seasonal | Football Stadium Sign is a higher-AOV hero product during Father's Day. |

**Benchmark:** AOV has been stable at £22–24. Increasing add-on attach on freestanding range is the clearest AOV lever not yet pulled.
