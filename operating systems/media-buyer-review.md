# Media Buyer Review: Daryl vs AI-Managed Ads

*Date: 2026-09-11*

## Summary

At £1,000/month, the current media buyer (Daryl) is a bottleneck rather than a capability multiplier. The evidence — particularly the diffuser launch week — shows budget decisions that actively hurt performance. The recommendation is to drop the external media buyer after proving the AI-managed restructure over one week.

## What £1,000/month Currently Buys

- Someone to log into Ads Manager and leave winning ads running
- Occasional budget changes that hurt performance
- A bottleneck on launching new products
- Slower response time than AI-managed operations

## What AI-Managed Ads Deliver Instead

- **Morning digest as decision engine** — data, analysis, and action before coffee
- **Instant creative feedback** — whether it fits, where to run it, what budget to start at
- **Same-day new product testing** — no waiting for Daryl's schedule
- **Smarter spend allocation** — no fragmenting spend across 6 ad sets on 2 daily purchases
- **£12,000/year back in pocket**

## The Diffuser Week: Case Study

Creative launched that sold 8 units in a day. Daryl then:
1. Raised the budget 52%
2. Split spend across untested ad sets
3. Performance went to zero

This is not bad luck — it is someone who does not understand what they are managing.

## Transition Plan

1. **Execute the Facebook restructure first** — pause the 14 ad sets, consolidate budget
2. **Run for one week** with morning digest tracking results
3. **Target: ROAS climbing toward 3x** on the consolidated structure
4. **If confirmed** — the proof is in hand that the problem was management, not the market
5. **Then drop Daryl** — the conversation writes itself with data backing the decision

## Risk Assessment

Risk of dropping Daryl is close to zero because:
- No capability is lost — everything he does, AI already does or does better with data
- Everything he does not do (creative, monitoring, new products) he was never doing anyway
- The restructure proves the thesis before any personnel change
