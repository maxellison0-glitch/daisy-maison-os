## Visual Learnings

- **Winning scene template (Father's Day 2026):** sage green wall, rustic dark wood floating shelf, pampas grass in glass vase (left), white pillar candle (centre), small ceramic vase (right). Warm candlelight. Cosy British home interior. Sign mounted flat and centred. 9:16 vertical.
- AI image text fidelity risk: always use reference upload (not description alone) when sign text must be exact.

### Sign Realism Formula (use in every Higgsfield prompt)
Extracted from the FD prompt that reliably produced photorealistic acrylic signs:

> *"flat UV-printed acrylic, no raised frame, no physical border — the coloured border is printed onto the surface, not a physical edge. [X]cm wide by 12cm tall, slim and flat. Shot on Sony A7 IV, 50mm f/2.8. Warm colour grade, film grain, photorealistic."*

- **"no raised frame, no physical border"** — critical. Prevents AI generating unwanted 3D or embossed borders.
- **"the coloured border is printed onto the surface"** — reinforces flat acrylic, not a picture frame.
- **"slim and flat"** — reinforces the thin 3mm profile.
- **Sony A7 IV, 50mm f/2.8** — specific camera/lens grounds the output as real photography.
- Dimensions always vary by product (teacher sign: 30cm × 12cm; large FD sign: 57cm × 12cm) but formula is otherwise constant.
